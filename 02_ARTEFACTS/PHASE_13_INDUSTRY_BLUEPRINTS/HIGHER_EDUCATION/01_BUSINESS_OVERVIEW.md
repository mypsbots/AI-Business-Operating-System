# Higher Education Business Overview

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [Higher Education Pack](./README.md) · [Phase 13 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_13_INDUSTRY_BLUEPRINTS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Industry:** Higher Education  •  **Document:** Business Overview

## Executive Summary

Universities deliver teaching, research and operations at scale, balancing academic quality, research output and student experience. This blueprint pack gives a reusable, vendor-neutral path to deploy AI across Higher Education with proven architecture, governance and delivery guidance.

## Business Challenges

- Student success and retention at scale.
- Administrative complexity across faculties.
- Research administration and compliance.

## Business Goals

- Improve retention and outcomes.
- Streamline administration.
- Accelerate and support research.

## Business Capability Map

- **Student success assistant** capability enabled by AI.
- **Research literature assistant** capability enabled by AI.
- **Admissions automation** capability enabled by AI.
- **Grant/admin drafting** capability enabled by AI.
- **Retention analytics** capability enabled by AI.

## Target Operating Model

- AI agents augment teams under human oversight.
- Shared platform: data, RAG, agents, automation, governance.
- Centre of excellence sets standards; teams deliver use cases.
- Continuous measurement against the success metrics.
