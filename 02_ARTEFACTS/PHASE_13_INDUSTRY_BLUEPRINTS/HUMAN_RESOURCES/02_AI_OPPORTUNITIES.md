# Human Resources AI Opportunities

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [Human Resources Pack](./README.md) · [Phase 13 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_13_INDUSTRY_BLUEPRINTS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Industry:** Human Resources  •  **Document:** AI Opportunities

## Executive Summary

The highest-value AI opportunities for Human Resources, prioritised for impact and feasibility.

## High-Value AI Use Cases

| Use Case | Description | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HR assistant | Answer employee questions via RAG over policy. | High |
| Resume screening | Assist screening (bias-checked, human-approved). | High |
| Onboarding automation | Automate onboarding workflows. | High |
| Attrition prediction | Identify retention risk. | High |
| Policy drafting | Draft HR documents. | High |

## RAG Opportunities

- Ground answers in Human Resources knowledge sources with citations.
- Enable semantic search across documents and records.
- Keep responses current via scheduled re-indexing.

## MCP Opportunities

- Expose core systems as governed MCP tools for agents.
- Enforce least-privilege scopes and human approval.
- Audit every tool invocation.

## Customer Experience Improvements

- Faster, 24/7, personalised service.
- Consistent, accurate, grounded answers.
- Proactive outreach based on predictions.

## Internal Productivity Gains

- Automate repetitive, rules-based work.
- Assist experts with drafting and synthesis.
- Reduce cycle times across core processes.

## Prioritisation (Value vs Effort)

| HR assistant | High | Medium | 1 |
| Resume screening | High | Medium | 2 |
