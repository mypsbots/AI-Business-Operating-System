# Non-profit Organisations Implementation Roadmap

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [Non-profit Organisations Pack](./README.md) · [Phase 13 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_13_INDUSTRY_BLUEPRINTS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Industry:** Non-profit Organisations  •  **Document:** Implementation Roadmap

## Executive Summary

A phased, low-risk path to deploy AI across Non-profit Organisations and scale what works.

## Adoption Roadmap

| Horizon | Focus | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 days | Discover and assess; pick 1–2 use cases | Baseline, data access and a prioritised backlog |
| 31–90 days | Build and pilot with guardrails | Validated pilot with measured impact |
| 3–6 months | Harden and integrate | Production rollout of initial use cases |
| 6–12 months | Scale across the portfolio | Multiple use cases live; CoE established |

## Change Management

- Engage stakeholders and communicate early.
- Train teams; redesign processes around AI + humans.
- Manage expectations with transparent metrics.

## Risks

- Starting too broad instead of proving value.
- Weak data foundations or access.
- Insufficient oversight on regulated decisions.
- Change resistance and unclear ownership.

## Dependencies

- Data access and quality.
- Platform, security and governance readiness.
- Skilled people and executive sponsorship.
