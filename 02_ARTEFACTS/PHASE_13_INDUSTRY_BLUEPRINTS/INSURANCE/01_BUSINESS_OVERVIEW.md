# Insurance Business Overview

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [Insurance Pack](./README.md) · [Phase 13 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_13_INDUSTRY_BLUEPRINTS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Industry:** Insurance  •  **Document:** Business Overview

## Executive Summary

Insurers price and manage risk, where underwriting, claims and fraud are data- and document-intensive. This blueprint pack gives a reusable, vendor-neutral path to deploy AI across Insurance with proven architecture, governance and delivery guidance.

## Business Challenges

- Manual, slow claims and underwriting.
- Fraud and leakage.
- Fairness and regulatory scrutiny.

## Business Goals

- Speed up claims and underwriting.
- Reduce fraud and leakage.
- Ensure fair, compliant decisions.

## Business Capability Map

- **Automated underwriting** capability enabled by AI.
- **Claims triage** capability enabled by AI.
- **Fraud detection** capability enabled by AI.
- **Document understanding** capability enabled by AI.
- **Customer assistant** capability enabled by AI.

## Target Operating Model

- AI agents augment teams under human oversight.
- Shared platform: data, RAG, agents, automation, governance.
- Centre of excellence sets standards; teams deliver use cases.
- Continuous measurement against the success metrics.
