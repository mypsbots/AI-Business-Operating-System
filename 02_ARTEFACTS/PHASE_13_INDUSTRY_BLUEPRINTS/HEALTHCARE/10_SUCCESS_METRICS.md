# Healthcare Success Metrics

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [Healthcare Pack](./README.md) · [Phase 13 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_13_INDUSTRY_BLUEPRINTS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Industry:** Healthcare  •  **Document:** Success Metrics

## Executive Summary

How to measure the impact of AI in Healthcare against business outcomes.

## KPIs

| Metric | Target Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Documentation time | Decrease |
| No-show rate | Decrease |
| Claim denial rate | Decrease |
| Patient satisfaction | Increase |
| Clinician burnout | Decrease |

## ROI Metrics

- Cost savings from automation and efficiency.
- Revenue uplift from better experience and decisions.
- Payback period and net benefit per use case.

## Cost Considerations

- Model/compute (token) costs — align to usage.
- Platform, data and integration costs.
- Run/oversight costs (human-in-the-loop, monitoring).

## Measurement Approach

- Baseline before deployment; measure deltas.
- Instrument outcomes, not just outputs.
- Review on a cadence and iterate.
