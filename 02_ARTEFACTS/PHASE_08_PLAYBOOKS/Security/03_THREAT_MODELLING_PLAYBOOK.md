# Threat Modelling Playbook

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Playbooks](../README.md) · [Phase 08 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_08_PLAYBOOKS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Playbook ID:** PLAY-000022  •  **Category:** Security

## Executive Summary

A repeatable, automatable workflow for **Threat Modelling**. Systematically identifying how a system could be attacked and deciding mitigations before building. It is executable by human teams and AI agents with clear human-approval points.

## Business Objective

Make threat modelling consistent, fast, safe and auditable across the enterprise.

## Scope

Covers the end-to-end threat modelling workflow. Out-of-scope concerns are delegated to their own playbooks.

## Trigger Conditions

- New design
- Major change
- Handling new sensitive data

## Preconditions

- Required inputs, access and approvals are available.
- Relevant rules and checklists are known.

## Inputs

- The request/context that triggered the workflow.
- Relevant data, artefacts and configuration.

## Outputs

- The completed outcome of the workflow.
- Evidence, audit trail and any follow-up actions.

## Roles Responsible

- Security
- Team
- Team + Security

## AI Agents Involved

- Task agent(s) that execute eligible steps.
- Reviewer/evaluator agent that checks outputs.
- Orchestrator that coordinates and escalates.

## Human Approval Points

- Before any high-risk or irreversible action.
- At the final approval/sign-off step.

## Workflow Overview

The workflow proceeds through 4 main steps with a decision point that routes high-risk actions to human approval.

## Mermaid Workflow Diagram

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    S([Trigger]) --> A0
    A0["Team: Diagram the system and data flows."]
    A1["Team + Security: Enumerate threats (STRIDE)."]
    A0 --> A1
    A2["Team: Decide mitigations or accept risk."]
    A1 --> A2
    A3["Security: Review and sign off."]
    A2 --> A3
    A3 --> D{High-risk?}
    D -->|Yes| H[Human approval]
    D -->|No| E[Execute]
    H --> E --> R([Record audit trail])
```

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Actor | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Team | Diagram the system and data flows. |
| 2 | Team + Security | Enumerate threats (STRIDE). |
| 3 | Team | Decide mitigations or accept risk. |
| 4 | Security | Review and sign off. |

## Decision Branches

- If the action is high-risk or irreversible -> require human approval.
- If validation fails -> remediate and repeat the step.
- If blocked -> follow the escalation path.

## Exception Handling

- Capture the error and context.
- Apply the documented fallback or safe-stop.
- Open an incident if customer impact is possible.

## Escalation Path

Owner -> domain lead -> Governance Division, with severity-based timelines.

## KPIs

- % sensitive systems modelled
- Unmitigated threats

## SLAs

- Acknowledge triggers within 1 business day.
- Complete standard runs within the agreed target time.
- Resolve exceptions per severity SLA.

## Risks

- Unconsidered threats become incidents.

## Best Practices

- Model early.
- Use STRIDE.
- Revisit on change.

## Anti-patterns

- Skipping for 'small' changes.
- Listing threats without mitigations.

## Definition of Done

- Outcome achieved and validated.
- Audit trail recorded.
- Follow-up actions tracked.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Required checklist passed.
- Approvals obtained.
