# Operating model, monitoring and implementation roadmap

Company-wide scope: apply this reference to Lemuran Ltd’s corporate/internal matters, all services and engagements, and every current or future product as relevant. FAQai.app examples do not limit applicability. See [the Legal Team Charter](20_LEGAL_TEAM_CHARTER.md).

## RACI and decision authority — proposed POLICY

| Work | Responsible | Accountable/approval | Consult |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company records/filings | Company administrator/accountant | Director | Counsel on structural events |
| Tax/VAT/payroll | Accountant/finance | Director for submission/payment | Tax specialist for cross-border/relief |
| Privacy/DPIAs/transfers | Privacy lead | Director or delegated owner | DPO if appointed, counsel, security |
| Contracts/IP | Commercial/legal preparer | Authorised signatory | Counsel, product, finance, insurance |
| AI release/security | Product/engineering/security | Product risk owner/director | Privacy, affected customer, sector counsel |
| People | HR/engagement manager | Director | Employment adviser/payroll |
| Complaints/incidents | Customer support/incident lead | Relevant owner and deputy | Legal/privacy/insurer |
| Knowledge/skill maintenance | Compliance maintainer | Governance owner | Functional reviewers |

One person may hold several roles in a small business, but role names do not imply existing staff. Name an external solicitor, accountant and insurance broker where expertise is needed. Do not make a statutory DPO accountable for management's business decisions or give them conflicting responsibilities.

## Company-wide operating backbone

Start with the matter register, product/service portfolio, controlled-document register and authority matrix in [file 20](20_LEGAL_TEAM_CHARTER.md). Maintain one company-level legal backlog, with scope-specific owners and documents. Every new product, service, engagement and internal legal issue enters this function.

## First 30 days

**Days 1–5:** verify entity and deadlines; inventory current terms/vendors/data; identify immediately overdue matters; designate owners/deputies; record VAT and ICO-fee status. Test the breach/rights/complaints inbox routing. These actions precede cosmetic policy drafting.

**Days 6–15:** complete purpose-level data map and supplier DPAs/transfers; inspect checkout/cookies/marketing; close IP-assignment gaps; draft separate SaaS and agency agreements; reconcile promises with architecture and insurance.

**Days 16–30:** approve priority templates/controls; run launch and incident exercises; implement calendar and evidence store; generate skills as drafts with the tests in file 16. Release only those whose prerequisites and human decisions are complete.

## Next 60 days

Complete retention/deletion tests, country/sector screening, people onboarding, security restore/tenant-isolation tests and vendor exit plans. Run sample contract and SOW reviews using real redacted facts. Adopt source monitoring, change triage and an exception register. Plan proportionate certification only if business needs justify it.

## Review cadence — internal defaults, not statutory deadlines

| Cadence/trigger | Checks | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Daily when operating | Incident/complaint/rights queues, legal notices, failed deadlines | Escalation and owner |
| Monthly | VAT rolling turnover/forecast, invoice/contract obligations, overdue actions, source watch | Reviewed action list |
| Quarterly | Vendor/transfer list, permissions, AI risks, claims, retention samples | Control evidence and gaps |
| Annually and on renewal | Accounts/confirmation statement/tax as actual due dates; ICO fee; policies; insurance; training | Approved calendar and renewal evidence |
| Every material change | New model/vendor/data purpose/tool, customer country, regulated sector, employee, pricing or terms | Focused reassessment before affected action |

Schedule dates from actual obligation records. A monthly meeting cannot defer a 72-hour incident report or a near-term filing. Use event alerts and deputy escalation.

## Source monitoring and legal change control

Watch Companies House reform guidance; HMRC rates/thresholds/filing changes; ICO DUAA implementation and guidance; CMA subscription and unfair-trading guidance; IPO AI/copyright; employment commencement guidance; NCSC/Ofcom; EU Commission/EUR-Lex where EU activity exists.

For each change: capture authority, publication/effective/commencement dates, territorial and transitional scope, old/new proposition, affected controls/skills/templates, reviewer and action deadline. Guidance, consultation and legislation must remain distinct. Mark uncertain commencement `unknown`; retrieve the instrument before turning a watch item into a mandatory control. Do not silently update signed contracts or live notices from a crawler.

**Proposed freshness policy:** recheck decisive sources at point of filing/signing/release; monthly for fast-changing tax, commencement and AI issues; quarterly for stable background. `verified_on` is not a promise of future correctness. A source outage produces a review task, not an automatic green status.

## Evidence and metrics

Measure overdue legal tasks, unknown high-risk facts, unsupported public promises, supplier-DPA coverage, rights response timeliness, incident decisions within the statutory window, training/evaluation completion, successful deletions/restores and stale-source count. “100% compliant” is not a useful metric without a defined scope and evidence.

Keep facts, obligations, tasks, approvals and evidence separate. Evidence pointers may link to access-controlled records; no company secrets, credentials, raw identity documents or customer datasets in a broadly indexed Git repository.

## Exceptions and urgent escalation

Record scope, reason, risk, compensating controls, owner, expiry and reviewer for POLICY exceptions. Never permit a director to “waive GDPR” or override consumer rights. Legal applicability disputes require counsel; commercial deviations require signatory approval and any needed counterparty agreement. For urgent statutory work, alert deputy/director immediately and prepare the review package in parallel; record delays and never imply a deadline has moved.

Acceptance: named ownership, prioritised backlog, documented source watch, and exercised escalation path. This pack creates no live scheduled automation and sends no external messages.
