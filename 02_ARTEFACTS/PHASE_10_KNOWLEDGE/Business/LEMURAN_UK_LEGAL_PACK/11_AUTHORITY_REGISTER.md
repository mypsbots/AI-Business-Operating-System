# Primary authority register and verification log

Company-wide scope: apply this reference to Lemuran Ltd’s corporate/internal matters, all services and engagements, and every current or future product as relevant. FAQai.app examples do not limit applicability. See [the Legal Team Charter](20_LEGAL_TEAM_CHARTER.md).

## Verification method and limits

All access attempts and search checks below occurred on **2026-10-09**. `opened` means the primary page was fetched; `search_extract` means an indexed primary-source excerpt was consulted, not a complete page review. `reference_only` is a relevant legal anchor supplied for later checking, **not verified text**. `fetch_failed` means the direct page was unavailable to this research tool. No row claims a solicitor's legal opinion. Guidance is a primary authority publication, but is not itself the underlying binding statute.

Before acting, check revised legislation, amendments, commencement orders, territorial extent and transitional provisions. Search snippets and policy announcements alone are insufficient to resolve disputed scope. Record contradictions rather than selecting the most convenient statement. A URL can be valid while content is stale. Do not use the withdrawn UK VAT MOSS page as current OSS guidance.
