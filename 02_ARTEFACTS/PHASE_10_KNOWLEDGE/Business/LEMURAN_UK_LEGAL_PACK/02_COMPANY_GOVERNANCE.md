# Company governance and Companies House

Company-wide scope: apply this reference to Lemuran Ltd’s corporate/internal matters, all services and engagements, and every current or future product as relevant. FAQai.app examples do not limit applicability. See [the Legal Team Charter](20_LEGAL_TEAM_CHARTER.md).

## Executive summary and responsibilities

The director remains responsible even where an accountant, solicitor or agent performs administrative work. Company ownership, decision authority and legal-entity details underpin every product and agency agreement. Sources C01–C07 in [the register](11_AUTHORITY_REGISTER.md).

## Binding controls

| ID / class | Trigger and requirement | Evidence/output |
|---|---|---|
| GOV-01 LAW | Maintain appropriate registered office and registered email; keep filings and required company information current | Company master record; reconciliation with public register |
| GOV-02 LAW | Prepare/file accounts, maintain records, and submit confirmation statements even when relevant obligations continue during dormancy | Calendar, approved accounts, receipts |
| GOV-03 LAW | Confirmation statement at least every 12 months; filing window normally 14 days after review period ends | Review date, due date and receipt; changes needing separate forms tracked separately |
| GOV-04 CONDITIONAL LAW | Meet current director/PSC identity-verification and statement requirements | Verified status and role-specific deadline; no personal code in ordinary logs |
| GOV-05 LAW | Meet directors' duties under Companies Act 2006, including conflicts and reasonable care; creditor interests become material in distress | Decisions, declared interests and escalation record |
| GOV-06 LAW | Use company disclosures on business letters, order forms and website: registered name, number, office and registration jurisdiction as applicable | Footer/invoice/terms checklist |
| GOV-07 CONDITIONAL LAW | Follow articles, shareholder rights, resolutions and filing duties on shares, appointments, registered details and structural changes | Approved event checklist and statutory receipts |
| GOV-08 LAW | Dividends only from profits available for distribution; record required approvals and vouchers; distinguish salary, expenses and director loans | Accounts-supported distribution record |

## Calendar rules

For an ordinary private limited company, annual accounts are generally due nine months after financial year end; first accounts generally 21 months from incorporation. Corporation Tax payment and return deadlines are different. Shortening accounting periods, first periods, extensions and other exceptions require a company-specific check. Use the actual Companies House/HMRC dates and accountant approval before finalising reminders. [Accounts deadlines](https://www.gov.uk/prepare-file-annual-accounts-for-limited-company).

Mandatory identity verification began on 18 November 2025 with a 12-month transition for existing directors/PSCs. Existing directors ordinarily supply their personal code through the relevant confirmation statement; PSC obligations have distinct windows and may require a separate submission even for the same person. Do not use 18 November 2026 as everybody's deadline. Retrieve the relevant role-specific deadline and current guidance. [Identity checks](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verify-your-identity-for-companies-house).

## Governance design — proposed POLICY/PRACTICE

- Appoint a director owner and deputy for filings; accountant prepares financial submissions. A second person checks dates and material representations.
- Maintain an authority matrix: contract signature, discounts, refunds, supplier spend, bank changes, IP assignments, risk exceptions and incident notifications.
- Keep board decisions, conflicts, shareholder consents, share issues/transfers and beneficial ownership evidence. Do not blindly reproduce obsolete local-register requirements: Companies House reforms changed register arrangements; check each register type.
- Separate corporate funds from personal spending and maintain director loan records. Forecast cash and escalate inability to pay debts; do not let an agent continue trading or distribute cash on a speculative solvency conclusion.
- Review sanctions/bribery exposure in procurement and sales. Company-wide fraud/modern slavery duties may depend on thresholds and business scope; avoid imposing large-company rules on an unassessed small company.

## Filing workflow and human gate

Retrieve authoritative company details → reconcile private records → calculate candidate deadline → obtain preparer/reviewer approval → show exact filing data and fee → authorised submission → save receipt → verify accepted status. A submitted form is not necessarily accepted. On uncertain outcome, check status before retrying to avoid duplicate filings. No automatic alteration of shareholders, PSCs, officers or bank details.

## Checklist and tests

- [ ] Company identifiers verified; no inferred registration number.
- [ ] Individual director and PSC duties evaluated separately.
- [ ] Accounts, confirmation statement and tax clocks separated.
- [ ] Director approval and filing receipt stored securely.
- [ ] Rejected/overdue filings create a priority escalation.

Test: a director who is also a PSC has verified identity but no PSC statement evidence. Output must flag the separate PSC requirement rather than mark the company complete. Test: dormant company does not become exempt from all Companies House filings.

Verification: 2026-10-09. Risks: missed dates, inaccurate public records, unauthorised commitments. Acceptance: evidence-backed calendar and authority matrix, with unresolved fields blocking only dependent actions.
