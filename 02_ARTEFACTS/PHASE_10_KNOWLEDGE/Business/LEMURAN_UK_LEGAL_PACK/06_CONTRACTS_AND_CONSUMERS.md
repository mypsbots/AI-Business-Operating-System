# Commercial contracts and consumer protection

Company-wide scope: apply this reference to Lemuran Ltd’s corporate/internal matters, all services and engagements, and every current or future product as relevant. FAQai.app examples do not limit applicability. See [the Legal Team Charter](20_LEGAL_TEAM_CHARTER.md).

## Scope and authority

Commercial contracts allocate deliverables, price and risk; they cannot contract out of mandatory rights. Counsel approves legal deviations and director approves commitments. Some written terms are legally required (such as processor clauses); the labels “MSA”, “SOW” and “SLA” themselves are commercial practice, not universal statutory forms. Sources K01–K05, P04 and IP01; [drafting schedules](13_CONTRACT_TEMPLATES.md).

## Contract review controls — POLICY unless underlying law or executed contract applies

Verify parties and signatory authority; define scope, dependencies and acceptance tests; specify fees/VAT/usage charges, payment, renewal and termination; establish document precedence; allocate IP and customer-data rights; align warranties and support with demonstrated capability. Review indemnities, caps, exclusions, audit rights, security, data residency, subcontractors, insurance, change control, dispute forum and exit assistance.

No liability number is intrinsically compliant. Negotiate on contract value, foreseeable loss, data exposure, insurance and enforceability. Do not exclude fraud, mandatory consumer rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including death/personal injury caused by negligence. Other B2B exclusions may be subject to statutory reasonableness tests. Avoid making service credits the exclusive remedy for every possible wrong. [Unfair Contract Terms Act](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1977/50/contents) and [fair contract guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers).

## FAQai.app provisions

Define authorised users, tenants, permitted material, rate limits, fair usage, support hours, plan changes and suspension. Explain material AI limitations and human escalation. Customer obligations should require authority to upload content and configure permitted uses without falsely transferring all responsibility to the customer. Describe whether content can train models only after verifying the actual arrangement.

Capture the accepted terms/order version, user, authority representation, timestamp and price. Provide accessible terms before purchase and durable confirmation where required. Changes to terms require a lawful mechanism and appropriate notice, not a hidden website edit. At exit, implement agreed data export, deletion, backups and billing cessation.

## Agency provisions

SOW includes in/out of scope, deliverables, milestones, customer access/dependencies, acceptance criteria, acceptance window and defect process. Distinguish an acceptance test from an aspirational business outcome. A change request states cost/timing/security/data implications and becomes effective only through the agreed approval process. Identify ownership of customer-specific work versus reusable Lemuran libraries and third-party components. Handover covers accounts, source/configuration, credentials through a secure channel, documentation, training and ongoing support owner.

## Consumer duties — LAW / CONDITIONAL LAW

If contracting with consumers, apply the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (quality/fitness/description and remedies for digital content; reasonable care and skill for services; fairness/transparency), the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and relevant DMCC provisions. Classify SaaS supply correctly: a mixed service/digital-content offering needs advice rather than a blanket “no refunds” rule.

Give required pre-contract information, total price including mandatory charges/taxes, trader contact details, duration/renewal, termination and relevant cancellation information. Distance contracts generally have a 14-day cancellation period, subject to statutory exceptions. Early service performance and digital-content supply have different request/consent/acknowledgement conditions and consequences. Clicking “start now” does not automatically extinguish all rights. Failure to provide cancellation information can extend exposure. [Distance selling](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses).

Unfair-commercial-practice provisions under DMCC apply from 6 April 2025; they include fake-review and drip-pricing protections. If publishing consumer reviews, assess duties to prevent/remove prohibited reviews. [CMA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unfair-commercial-practices-cma207/unfair-commercial-practices).

**WATCH:** new DMCC subscription-contract rules are expected in spring 2027 according to the checked government guidance. Do not describe them as already operational in October 2026. Design for clear reminders, easy cancellation and applicable cooling-off rights, but recheck legislation and commencement before stating exact future duties. [Government update](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/consumers-to-save-around-400-million-every-year-from-government-crackdown-on-costly-subscription-traps).

## Approval and tests

Low-risk drafts can be prepared without interrupting the user. Signing, publishing final terms and changing live billing/cancellation behaviour require the relevant authorised approval. Store exact approved version and effective date; invalidate approval after material edits.

Tests: consumer checkout never inherits a B2B blanket refund exclusion; SOW lacking measurable acceptance returns unresolved fields; “99.99% uptime” without operational evidence is flagged; vendor retention conflicting with the customer DPA blocks that promise. Acceptance: signed/accepted version, capability evidence and obligation register. Verified 2026-10-09.
