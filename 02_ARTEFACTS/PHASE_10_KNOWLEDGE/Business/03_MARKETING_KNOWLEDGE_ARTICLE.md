# Marketing

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Executive Summary

Marketing creates awareness, demand and reputation by understanding the customer and communicating value through the right channels — now including visibility inside AI answer engines.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Marketing** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of marketing as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Positioning and messaging define how you are perceived.
- The funnel: awareness -> consideration -> conversion -> retention.
- Channels: content, SEO/AEO/GEO, paid, social, email, partnerships.
- Attribution connects spend to outcomes.
- Brand is the compounding asset of trust.

## Business Context

Understanding marketing helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, marketing is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Define audience, message and KPI before spending.
- Measure attribution and iterate.
- Optimise content for both search and AI answer engines.

## Anti-patterns

- Chasing vanity metrics.
- Unsubstantiated claims.
- Ignoring privacy/consent.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## FAQs

**Q: What is GEO?**

A: Generative Engine Optimization — making your brand visible and accurate inside AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

**Q: What is a marketing funnel?**

A: A model of the customer journey from awareness to retention.
