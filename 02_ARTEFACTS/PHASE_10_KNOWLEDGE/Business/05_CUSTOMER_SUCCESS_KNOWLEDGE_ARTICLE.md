# Customer Success

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Executive Summary

Customer Success proactively ensures customers achieve their desired outcomes, driving adoption, retention and expansion.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Customer Success** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of customer success as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Onboarding sets the foundation for value.
- Health scores predict churn and expansion.
- Net revenue retention is the key SaaS growth lever.
- QBRs align on value and next steps.
- Proactive beats reactive.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding customer success helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, customer success is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Define success outcomes with the customer.
- Act on health signals early.
- Run reviews for key accounts.

## Anti-patterns

- Reactive-only support.
- No defined success outcomes.
- Ignoring early churn signals.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real customer success task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is NRR?**

A: Net Revenue Retention — revenue retained plus expansion from existing customers; >100% means growth without new logos.

**Q: What is a health score?**

A: A composite signal (usage, sentiment, support) predicting churn/expansion.
