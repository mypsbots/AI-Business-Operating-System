# Legal

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

The legal function manages contracts, intellectual property, privacy and regulatory obligations to reduce risk and enable the business safely.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Legal** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of legal as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Contracts allocate risk and obligations.
- IP (copyright, trademarks, patents) protects assets.
- Privacy law (GDPR, CCPA) governs personal data.
- AI regulation (EU AI Act) adds new obligations.
- Compliance and records support audits.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding legal helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, legal is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Review contracts before signing.
- Respect third-party licences and IP.
- Build privacy and AI compliance in by design.

## Anti-patterns

- Signing unreviewed contracts.
- Ignoring open-source licences.
- Treating compliance as an afterthought.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real legal task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is the EU AI Act?**

A: A risk-based EU regulation governing AI systems, with obligations scaled to risk level.

**Q: What is DPA?**

A: A Data Processing Agreement governing how a vendor handles personal data.
