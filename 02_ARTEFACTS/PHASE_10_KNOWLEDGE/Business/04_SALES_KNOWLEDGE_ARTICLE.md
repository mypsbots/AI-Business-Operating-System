# Sales

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

Sales is the disciplined process of qualifying, advancing and closing opportunities by helping the right customers solve real problems.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Sales** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of sales as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Qualification frameworks (MEDDIC, BANT) focus effort.
- Pipeline and forecasting predict revenue.
- Discovery uncovers the customer's real problem and decision process.
- Value selling beats feature dumping.
- Handover to Customer Success protects the relationship.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding sales helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, sales is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Qualify rigorously; disqualify early.
- Sell outcomes, tell the truth.
- Keep the CRM accurate for reliable forecasts.

## Anti-patterns

- Over-promising to close.
- Stale or fictional CRM data.
- Skipping discovery.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real sales task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is MEDDIC?**

A: A B2B qualification framework: Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion.

**Q: Why does forecast accuracy matter?**

A: It drives hiring, investment and credibility with leadership.
