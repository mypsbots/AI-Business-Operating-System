# Operations

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

Business operations design and run the repeatable processes that turn strategy into reliable, efficient delivery across the organisation.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Operations** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of operations as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Process design and SOPs make work repeatable.
- Metrics and SLAs make performance visible.
- Automation removes toil and error.
- Continuous improvement (Lean, Kaizen) compounds gains.
- RACI clarifies ownership.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding operations helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, operations is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Document processes as SOPs and playbooks.
- Measure cycle time and quality.
- Automate repetitive, rules-based work.

## Anti-patterns

- Tribal knowledge with no documentation.
- Optimising locally while harming the whole.
- Automating a broken process.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real operations task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is an SOP?**

A: A Standard Operating Procedure — documented steps for a repeatable task.

**Q: What is RACI?**

A: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed — a responsibility model.
