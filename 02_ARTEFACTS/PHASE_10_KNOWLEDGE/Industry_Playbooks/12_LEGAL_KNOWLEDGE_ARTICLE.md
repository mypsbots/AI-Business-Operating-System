# Legal

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

The legal industry (firms and in-house teams) is document- and knowledge-intensive, making it a strong fit for AI with careful oversight.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Legal** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of legal as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Contract review and analysis.
- Legal research (RAG over case law/regulation).
- Document drafting and summarisation.
- E-discovery and review.
- Matter and knowledge management.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

AI is reshaping the Legal sector. The highest-value, lowest-risk entry points are listed under Core Concepts; the business case rests on efficiency, experience and risk reduction.

## Technical Context

Typical solutions combine grounded RAG over approved sources, workflow automation and predictive models, with human oversight for regulated decisions.

## Best Practices

- Start with a high-value, low-risk use case and a clear metric.
- Ground AI in approved, current sources (RAG) and cite them.
- Keep a human in the loop for regulated or high-impact decisions.
- Map and meet the industry's compliance obligations early.

## Anti-patterns

- Deploying AI into regulated decisions without oversight.
- Ignoring sector-specific compliance.
- Boiling the ocean instead of proving one use case.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Contract review assistant flagging risky clauses.
- Research assistant grounded in authoritative sources with citations.
- Drafting first-pass documents for lawyer review.

## Compliance Considerations

- Professional responsibility / privilege
- Confidentiality and privacy
- Unauthorised practice of law
- Accuracy and citation duties

## Key Challenges

- Accuracy and hallucination risk
- Confidentiality
- Professional liability

## FAQs

**Q: Where should this industry start with AI?**

A: With a narrow, high-value use case (often document or service automation) that has measurable ROI and low regulatory risk.

**Q: What is the biggest risk?**

A: Compliance and trust — regulated decisions need human oversight, auditability and grounded, citable answers.
