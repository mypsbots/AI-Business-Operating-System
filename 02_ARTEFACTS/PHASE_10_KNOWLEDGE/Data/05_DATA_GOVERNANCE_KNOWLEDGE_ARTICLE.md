# Data Governance

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

Data governance defines how data is owned, classified, secured and used so it is trustworthy, compliant and well-managed.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Data Governance** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of data governance as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Ownership, stewardship and a data catalog.
- Classification and access control.
- Privacy and regulatory compliance.
- Data quality standards.
- Lifecycle, retention and lineage.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding data governance helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, data governance is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Assign clear data ownership/stewardship.
- Classify and protect data by sensitivity.
- Catalog data with lineage and quality.

## Anti-patterns

- Ownerless 'dark data'.
- No classification or retention rules.
- Treating governance as bureaucracy, not enablement.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real data governance task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is a data steward?**

A: A person accountable for the quality, definitions and proper use of a data domain.

**Q: Why classify data?**

A: To apply the right protection, access and retention based on sensitivity.
