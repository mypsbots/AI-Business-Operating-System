# Analytics

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

Analytics turns raw data into insight to support decisions, spanning descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive questions.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Analytics** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of analytics as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Metrics, dimensions and KPIs.
- Event tracking and product analytics.
- Cohorts, funnels and retention analysis.
- Experimentation (A/B testing).
- Self-serve dashboards.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding analytics helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, analytics is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Define metrics precisely and consistently.
- Instrument events deliberately.
- Validate with experiments, not opinions.

## Anti-patterns

- Vanity metrics.
- Inconsistent metric definitions.
- HiPPO decisions (highest-paid person's opinion).

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real analytics task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: Descriptive vs predictive analytics?**

A: Descriptive explains what happened; predictive forecasts what will happen.

**Q: What is a cohort analysis?**

A: Grouping users by a shared trait/time to compare behaviour over time.
