# Retention

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Executive Summary

Retention measures whether users keep coming back and getting value — the foundation of sustainable SaaS growth.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Retention** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of retention as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Retention curves and flattening.
- Engagement loops and habits.
- Churn types: voluntary vs involuntary.
- Resurrection and re-engagement.
- NRR as the north-star for growth.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding retention helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, retention is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Track retention curves by cohort.
- Build engagement/habit loops.
- Reduce involuntary churn (failed payments).

## Anti-patterns

- Focusing on acquisition while leaking customers.
- Ignoring involuntary churn.
- No re-engagement strategy.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real retention task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is a good retention curve?**

A: One that flattens (stabilises) rather than decaying to zero, showing a sticky core.

**Q: Voluntary vs involuntary churn?**

A: Voluntary is users choosing to leave; involuntary is e.g. failed payments.
