# Onboarding

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Knowledge ID:** KN-000045  •  **Category:** SaaS

## Tags

`onboarding`, `activation`, `aha moment`, `time-to-value`, `checklist`

## Executive Summary

Onboarding guides new users to their first value (and habit) quickly, directly shaping activation and retention.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Onboarding** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of onboarding as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Aha moment and time-to-value.
- Activation milestones and checklists.
- Personalised, progressive onboarding.
- Empty states and contextual guidance.
- Measuring activation rate.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding onboarding helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, onboarding is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Get users to value fast.
- Use checklists and progressive disclosure.
- Measure and optimise activation.

## Anti-patterns

- Overwhelming first-run experiences.
- No clear first value.
- One-size-fits-all onboarding.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real onboarding task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is the aha moment?**

A: The point where a user first experiences the product's core value.

**Q: Why does onboarding matter for retention?**

A: Activated users who reach value early are far more likely to retain.
