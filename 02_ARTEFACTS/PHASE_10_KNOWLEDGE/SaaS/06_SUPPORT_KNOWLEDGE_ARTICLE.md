# Support

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

**Knowledge ID:** KN-000047  •  **Category:** SaaS

## Tags

`support`, `SLA`, `CSAT`, `knowledge base`, `deflection`, `AI copilot`

## Executive Summary

Customer support resolves user issues quickly and empathetically, protecting satisfaction and retention — increasingly AI-assisted.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Support** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of support as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Tiered support and escalation.
- SLAs and response/resolution times.
- Knowledge base and deflection.
- CSAT and ticket analytics.
- AI copilots and deflection bots.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding support helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, support is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Set and meet clear SLAs.
- Invest in self-serve knowledge and deflection.
- Use AI to assist agents, with human oversight.

## Anti-patterns

- Slow, inconsistent responses.
- No knowledge base.
- Letting AI answer unsupervised on sensitive issues.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real support task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: What is ticket deflection?**

A: Resolving issues via self-serve content/AI before they become tickets.

**Q: How is support measured?**

A: Via CSAT, first-response and resolution time, and deflection rate.
