# Pricing

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Tags

## Executive Summary

Pricing and packaging determine how a SaaS captures the value it creates — one of the highest-leverage growth levers.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Pricing** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of pricing as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Pricing models: flat, tiered, usage-based, seat-based, hybrid.
- Value metric alignment (charge for what scales with value).
- Packaging and good-better-best tiers.
- Price localisation and discounting discipline.
- Usage-based pricing for AI cost pass-through.

Understanding pricing helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, pricing is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Anchor pricing to a value metric.
- Test and iterate pricing deliberately.
- Align AI usage costs with usage-based pricing.

## Anti-patterns

- Cost-plus pricing that ignores value.
- Too many confusing tiers.
- Unstructured discounting.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## FAQs

**Q: What is a value metric?**

A: The unit you charge for that grows with the value the customer gets (e.g. seats, API calls).

**Q: Why usage-based pricing for AI?**

A: It aligns revenue with variable model/compute costs that scale with usage.
