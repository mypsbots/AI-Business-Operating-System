# Product-Led Growth

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Executive Summary

Product-Led Growth (PLG) uses the product itself as the primary driver of acquisition, conversion and expansion.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Product-Led Growth** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of product-led growth as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Free trial vs freemium models.
- Time-to-value and aha moments.
- In-product activation and onboarding.
- Self-serve conversion and expansion.
- Product-qualified leads (PQLs).

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding product-led growth helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, product-led growth is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Optimise time-to-value relentlessly.
- Instrument activation and PQLs.
- Make self-serve upgrade frictionless.

## Anti-patterns

- Long time-to-value.
- Gating core value behind sales.
- No activation instrumentation.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real product-led growth task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: Freemium vs free trial?**

A: Freemium offers a permanently free tier; a trial gives full access for a limited time.

**Q: What is a PQL?**

A: A Product-Qualified Lead — a user whose in-product behaviour signals buying intent.
