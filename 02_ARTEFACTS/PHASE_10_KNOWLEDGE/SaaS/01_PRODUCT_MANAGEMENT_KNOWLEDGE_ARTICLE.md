# Product Management

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Knowledge](../README.md) · [Phase 10 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_10_KNOWLEDGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Executive Summary

Product management discovers customer problems and decides what to build to deliver value, balancing desirability, viability and feasibility.

## Purpose

To give people and AI agents a clear, reusable explanation of **Product Management** that supports learning, retrieval (RAG) and decision support.

## Scope

Covers the core ideas, practices and pitfalls of product management as applied in AIBOS. Deep procedural detail lives in the related playbooks.

## Core Concepts

- Discovery and validation before build.
- Prioritisation (RICE, value vs effort).
- Roadmaps and outcomes over outputs.
- Metrics: activation, retention, NPS.
- Stakeholder alignment and communication.

## Definitions

See the [Enterprise Glossary](../../../00_ROOT/AIBOS_ENTERPRISE_GLOSSARY.md).

## Business Context

Understanding product management helps teams make better decisions, reduce risk and move faster. It connects directly to the related rules, checklists and playbooks below.

## Technical Context

In AIBOS, product management is applied through the related standards and automated wherever possible, with AI agents operating under human oversight.

## Best Practices

- Validate problems before building.
- Prioritise by impact; say no.
- Measure outcomes, not features shipped.

## Anti-patterns

- Feature factory.
- Building without validation.
- No success metric.

## Design Patterns

Apply the best practices above as repeatable patterns for this topic.

## Examples

- Apply the best practices above to a real product management task and validate with the related checklist.

## FAQs

**Q: Outputs vs outcomes?**

A: Outputs are what you ship; outcomes are the customer/business results that actually matter.

**Q: What is RICE?**

A: Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort — a prioritisation score.
