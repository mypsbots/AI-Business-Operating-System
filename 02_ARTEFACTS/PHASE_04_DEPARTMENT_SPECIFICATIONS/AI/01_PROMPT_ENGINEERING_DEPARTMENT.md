This document defines **Prompt Engineering Department** within the AIBOS Enterprise v2.0 framework (Phase 04). It inherits every root standard and is designed to be enterprise-ready, machine-readable and AI-friendly.

## Department Mission

To operate Prompt Engineering Department to a consistent, enterprise-grade standard that delivers measurable value while balancing autonomy with human oversight.

## Vision

Prompt Engineering Department contributes to an AI-native enterprise that is secure, governed, automated and continuously improving.

## Business Purpose

Prompt Engineering Department exists to deliver measurable value while upholding the AIBOS principles of security, privacy and accountability.
