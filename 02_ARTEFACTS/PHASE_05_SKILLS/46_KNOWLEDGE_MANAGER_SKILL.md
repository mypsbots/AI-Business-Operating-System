## Role Summary

**Mission:** Ensure the right knowledge is captured, trusted and reusable.

Owns how the organisation captures, organises and reuses knowledge — making it findable for people and high-quality for AI/RAG systems.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define knowledge taxonomy and standards.
- Curate, review and maintain knowledge bases.
- Ensure knowledge quality for AI/RAG grounding.
- Drive knowledge capture and reuse.
- Govern lifecycle, ownership and freshness.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Information architecture and taxonomy.
- Knowledge bases, CMS and metadata.
- Content quality for RAG/embeddings.

### Business Skills

- Driving a knowledge-sharing culture.

### AI Skills

- Preparing and governing knowledge for AI retrieval.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Confluence/Notion
- KB platforms
- Taxonomy tools
- Vector DBs (with RAG team)

## Certifications and Qualifications

- KM certifications
- AIBOS Knowledge certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: information architecture, taxonomy.
2. Intermediate: KM systems, governance, quality.
3. Advanced: knowledge engineering for AI/RAG.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Knowledge freshness
- Findability/search success
- Reuse rate
- RAG answer quality

## Typical Deliverables

- Taxonomy
- Curated knowledge base
- KM standards

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: RAG Engineer, Content Strategist, Support Engineer.

## Career Progression

1. **Specialist**
2. **Knowledge Manager**
3. **Senior**
4. **Head of Knowledge**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you structure knowledge for findability?
2. How do you keep a knowledge base from going stale?
3. How do you prepare knowledge for a RAG system?
