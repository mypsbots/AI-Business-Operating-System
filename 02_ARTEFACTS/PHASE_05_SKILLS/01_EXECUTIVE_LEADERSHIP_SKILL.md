## Role Summary

**Mission:** Align the organisation around a clear strategy and ensure it is executed safely, ethically and profitably.

Sets the vision, strategy and culture of the AI-native enterprise, allocates capital and people, and is accountable for outcomes, risk and governance across all divisions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and communicate enterprise vision, strategy and OKRs.
- Allocate budget, headcount and AI investment across divisions.
- Own enterprise risk, governance and regulatory accountability.
- Sponsor the AI operating model and responsible-AI policy.
- Build and develop the senior leadership team.
- Report performance to the board and shareholders.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Literacy in AI capabilities, limits and risks (enough to govern, not build).
- Financial modelling, unit economics and capital allocation.
- Reading data dashboards and interpreting KPIs/OKRs.

### Business Skills

- Corporate strategy and competitive positioning.
- P&L ownership, budgeting and investor relations.
- Organisational design and change leadership.
- Stakeholder and board management.

### AI Skills

- Responsible-AI governance and policy setting.
- Understanding where AI agents create value vs. risk.
- Using AI decision-support and executive copilots.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Board/EIS dashboards
- OKR platforms (Workboard, Ally)
- Financial planning (Anaplan, Pigment)
- AI executive copilots

## Certifications and Qualifications

- MBA or equivalent experience
- Board governance (IoD / NACD)
- AIBOS Executive AI Leadership certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: AI literacy for leaders, responsible-AI principles.
2. Intermediate: AI operating models, value cases, risk frameworks.
3. Advanced: enterprise-scale AI governance and transformation.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Revenue and margin vs. plan
- Enterprise OKR attainment
- Employee engagement / retention
- Risk and compliance posture

## Typical Deliverables

- Enterprise strategy
- Annual operating plan
- Board reports
- Responsible-AI policy mandate

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Board, Division leaders, Investors, Governance Division.

## Career Progression

1. **Function lead**
2. **VP / SVP**
3. **C-suite executive**
4. **CEO / President**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you decide where to invest in AI vs. defer?
2. Describe a time you balanced growth against risk.
3. How do you set and cascade OKRs across an organisation?
4. How do you govern AI use responsibly at enterprise scale?
