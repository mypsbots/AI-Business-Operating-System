## Role Summary

**Mission:** Prevent AI harm and keep AI behaviour within defined boundaries.

Ensures AI systems behave safely and within policy — red-teaming, guardrails and alignment controls to prevent harmful or non-compliant outputs.

## Key Responsibilities

- Red-team models and agents for misuse and jailbreaks.
- Design guardrails, content filters and policies.
- Implement human-in-the-loop and kill-switches.
- Assess and mitigate AI risks (bias, safety, security).
- Define responsible-AI policy and incident response.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Adversarial testing and red-teaming.
- Guardrail/filter frameworks; OWASP LLM Top 10.
- Alignment, bias and content-safety techniques.

### Business Skills

- AI risk, ethics and regulation (EU AI Act).

### AI Skills

- Jailbreak/prompt-injection defence, safety evaluation.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Guardrails AI/NeMo Guardrails
- Llama Guard
- Red-team frameworks

## Certifications and Qualifications

- AIBOS AI Safety certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: LLM risks, OWASP LLM Top 10.
2. Intermediate: guardrails, red-teaming, safety evals.
3. Advanced: alignment, governance and incident response.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Jailbreak success rate (lower is better)
- Unsafe-output rate
- Time to mitigate
- Policy coverage

## Typical Deliverables

- Red-team reports
- Guardrail configs
- Responsible-AI policies

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Security Engineer, AI Evaluation Engineer, Legal, Compliance Specialist.

## Career Progression

1. **Security/ML Engineer**
2. **AI Safety Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **Head of AI Safety**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you red-team an LLM application?
2. How do you design layered guardrails?
3. How do you respond to an AI safety incident?
