## Role Summary

**Mission:** Turn frontier research into validated, applicable capability.

Advances the organisation's capabilities through applied research — exploring new AI methods, validating them and transferring them to product.

## Key Responsibilities

- Survey literature and identify promising methods.
- Design and run rigorous experiments.
- Prototype and benchmark new techniques.
- Publish/internally share findings.
- Transfer validated methods to engineering.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Research methodology and experimentation.
- ML/AI depth (papers, math, implementation).
- Benchmarking and reproducibility.

### Business Skills

- Aligning research to strategic needs.

### AI Skills

- Frontier AI methods (LLMs, agents, training, evaluation).

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- PyTorch
- Jupyter
- arXiv
- Experiment tracking

## Certifications and Qualifications

- PhD/MSc or equivalent
- AIBOS Research certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: ML theory, math, methodology.
2. Intermediate: experimentation, benchmarking, implementation.
3. Advanced: original research and tech transfer.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Validated methods transferred
- Benchmark improvements
- Publications/IP

## Typical Deliverables

- Research reports
- Prototypes
- Benchmarks

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: ML Engineer, AI Engineers, Innovation Lead.

## Career Progression

1. **Research Engineer**
2. **Research Scientist**
3. **Senior**
4. **Principal**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you design a rigorous experiment?
2. How do you decide if a new method is worth adopting?
3. How do you transfer research into product?
