# The 22 Immutable Laws of Branding

> **Navigate:** [Home](../../../README.md) · [Catalogue](../../../ROOT_INDEX.md) · [All Skills](../README.md) · [Branding Skills](README.md) · [Phase 05 Prompt](../../../01_PHASES/PHASE_05_SKILLS_MASTER_PROMPT.md)

## Overview

This is the foundational reference for the AIBOS Branding skills. The 22 Immutable
Laws of Branding (Al Ries & Laura Ries) describe how brands are built and destroyed
in the customer's mind. Every branding skill in this folder is an application of one
or more of these laws. Use this document as the shared vocabulary and first principles
for brand strategy, and link to it from any brand work.

> **On quality and ethics:** these laws describe how perception works, not a licence
> to mislead. AIBOS requires that every brand claim be true, deliverable and
> substantiated (see [Perception Management](09_PERCEPTION_MANAGEMENT_SKILL.md)).

## The Laws

## Law 1: The Law of Expansion

A brand's power is inversely proportional to its scope; trying to be everything to everyone weakens it.

**Applied by skill(s):** Focus and Simplification.

## Law 2: The Law of Contraction

A brand becomes stronger when you narrow its focus; specialisation builds leadership.

**Applied by skill(s):** Focus and Simplification, Strategic Brand Extension, Differentiation and Ownership of Attributes, Creating New Categories.

## Law 3: The Law of Publicity

Brands are born with publicity, not advertising; being first to earn media builds the perception of leadership.

**Applied by skill(s):** Publicity and Media Relations, Creating New Categories.

## Law 4: The Law of Advertising

Advertising does not build a brand, it defends one; its job is to maintain a position publicity created.

**Applied by skill(s):** Creating Category Leadership, Publicity and Media Relations, Advertising and Promotion.

## Law 5: The Law of The Word

A brand should own a single word or idea in the prospect's mind.

**Applied by skill(s):** Brand Positioning, Naming Expertise, Consistency in Messaging, Perception Management, Differentiation and Ownership of Attributes, Competitor Analysis and Positioning, Consumer and Perception Research, Repositioning.

## Law 6: The Law of Credentials

An authentic proof of leadership (the 'credential') makes every other claim believable.

**Applied by skill(s):** Creating Category Leadership, Publicity and Media Relations, Perception Management.

## Law 7: The Law of Quality

Quality matters, but brands are not built on quality alone; they are built on perception and a narrow focus.

**Applied by skill(s):** Perception Management, Consumer and Perception Research.

## Law 8: The Law of The Category

A leading brand should promote the category, not just the brand; grow the category and you grow the leader.

**Applied by skill(s):** Brand Positioning, Creating Category Leadership, Advertising and Promotion, Collaborative Networking (Fellowship), Competitor Analysis and Positioning, Creating New Categories.

## Law 9: The Law of The Name

In the long run a brand is nothing more than a name; choose it with care.

**Applied by skill(s):** Naming Expertise.

## Law 10: The Law of Extensions

The easiest way to destroy a brand is to put its name on everything (line extension dilutes meaning).

**Applied by skill(s):** Strategic Brand Extension.

## Law 11: The Law of Fellowship

A brand should welcome competitors; a cluster of brands grows the category for everyone.

**Applied by skill(s):** Collaborative Networking (Fellowship).

## Law 12: The Law of The Generic

One of the fastest routes to failure is giving a brand a generic name; brands need distinctive names.

**Applied by skill(s):** Naming Expertise.

## Law 13: The Law of The Company

Brands are brands; the company is the company. The brand name should usually lead, not the company name.

**Applied by skill(s):** Building a Global Brand.

## Law 14: The Law of Subbrands

What branding builds, subbranding can destroy; sub-brands fragment focus if used carelessly.

**Applied by skill(s):** Strategic Brand Extension.

## Law 15: The Law of Siblings

There is a time and a place to launch a second brand — as a distinct sibling, not an extension.

**Applied by skill(s):** Strategic Brand Extension.

## Law 16: The Law of Shape

A brand's logotype should be designed to fit the eyes — both eyes, favouring a horizontal shape.

**Applied by skill(s):** General brand strategy.

## Law 17: The Law of Color

A brand should use a colour that is the opposite of its major competitor's.

**Applied by skill(s):** Competitor Analysis and Positioning, Repositioning.

## Law 18: The Law of Borders

There are no barriers to global branding; a brand should know no borders.

**Applied by skill(s):** Collaborative Networking (Fellowship), Building a Global Brand.

## Law 19: The Law of Consistency

A brand is built over years by staying consistent; do not change what a brand stands for.

**Applied by skill(s):** Consistency in Messaging, Advertising and Promotion, Building a Global Brand, Brand Monitoring and Adaptation, Crisis and Change Management, Persistence and Long-term Vision.

## Law 20: The Law of Change

Brands can be changed, but only rarely, and only very carefully.

**Applied by skill(s):** Consistency in Messaging, Brand Monitoring and Adaptation, Crisis and Change Management, Persistence and Long-term Vision, Repositioning.

## Law 21: The Law of Mortality

No brand lives forever; sometimes euthanasia (a graceful end) is the best option.

**Applied by skill(s):** Brand Monitoring and Adaptation, Crisis and Change Management, Persistence and Long-term Vision.

## Law 22: The Law of Singularity

The most important aspect of a brand is its single-minded focus on one attribute or idea.

**Applied by skill(s):** Focus and Simplification, Brand Positioning, Differentiation and Ownership of Attributes, Consumer and Perception Research.

## Vocabulary Cross-Map

Some summaries of these laws use different names (for example *Focus*, *Leader*,
*Opposite*, *Division*, *Persuasion*, *Uniqueness*, *Eternity*). They map to the
canonical laws above as follows:

| Term | Canonical law(s) |
| --- | --- |
| Law of Focus | Contraction (2) + Singularity (22) |
| Law of the Leader | The Category (8) + Credentials (6) |
| Law of the Opposite | Color (17) — position as the opposite of the leader |
| Law of Division | Contraction (2) + The Category (8) — categories divide over time |
| Law of Persuasion | Consistency (19) — persistent, consistent messaging |
| Law of Uniqueness | Singularity (22) + The Word (5) |
| Law of Eternity | Consistency (19) + Change (20) + Mortality (21) |
