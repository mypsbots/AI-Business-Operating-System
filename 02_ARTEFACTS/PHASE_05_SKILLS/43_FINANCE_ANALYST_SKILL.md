## Role Summary

**Mission:** Provide accurate financial insight and planning for decisions.

Analyses financial data, builds models and forecasts, and provides insight to guide business decisions and planning.

## Key Responsibilities

- Build financial models and forecasts.
- Analyse variance, costs and unit economics.
- Support budgeting and planning (FP&A).
- Produce management reports and KPIs.
- Advise on investment and pricing decisions.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Financial modelling and Excel.
- Accounting fundamentals and metrics.
- SQL/BI for financial data.

### Business Skills

- Linking finance to strategy and operations.

### AI Skills

- Using AI for forecasting and report automation.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Excel
- Anaplan/Pigment
- BI tools
- ERP

## Certifications and Qualifications

- CFA/ACCA/CIMA
- AIBOS Finance certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: accounting, Excel, financial statements.
2. Intermediate: modelling, FP&A, unit economics.
3. Advanced: strategic finance and AI-assisted FP&A.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Forecast accuracy
- Reporting timeliness
- Cost savings identified

## Typical Deliverables

- Financial models
- Forecasts
- Management reports

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Executive Leadership, Account Manager, Operations Manager.

## Career Progression

1. **Analyst**
2. **Senior Analyst**
3. **Finance Manager**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. Build a simple SaaS revenue model.
2. How do you analyse a budget variance?
3. What unit economics matter for SaaS?
