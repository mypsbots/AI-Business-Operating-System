## Role Summary

**Mission:** Make operations efficient, reliable and scalable.

Keeps the business running smoothly — designing and improving processes, coordinating teams and managing day-to-day operations.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and improve business processes.
- Coordinate cross-functional delivery.
- Manage vendors, tools and budgets.
- Track operational KPIs and SLAs.
- Drive automation and continuous improvement.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Process mapping and operations tooling.

### Business Skills

- Process design and optimisation (Lean).
- Project/program management.
- Vendor and budget management.

### AI Skills

- Identifying and deploying AI/automation in operations.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Project tools (Asana)
- Process tools
- BI dashboards

## Certifications and Qualifications

- PMP/Lean Six Sigma
- AIBOS Operations certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: process mapping, project basics.
2. Intermediate: Lean, KPIs, vendor management.
3. Advanced: operational strategy and AI automation.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Process cycle time
- Cost efficiency
- SLA attainment
- Automation coverage

## Typical Deliverables

- Process designs
- SOPs
- Operational dashboards

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: All divisions, Finance Analyst, Knowledge Manager.

## Career Progression

1. **Coordinator**
2. **Operations Manager**
3. **Senior**
4. **COO**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you improve a broken process?
2. How do you decide what to automate?
3. How do you manage competing priorities?
