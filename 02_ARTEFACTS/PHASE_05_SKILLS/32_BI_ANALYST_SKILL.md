## Role Summary

**Mission:** Give decision-makers clear, timely, trustworthy insight.

Turns data into dashboards, reports and insights that help the business make decisions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Build dashboards and reports.
- Analyse trends and answer business questions.
- Define KPIs with stakeholders.
- Ensure data accuracy and clear visualisation.
- Present insights and recommendations.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- SQL and BI tools (Power BI, Tableau, Looker).
- Data visualisation and storytelling.
- Basic statistics.

### Business Skills

- Understanding business KPIs and stakeholders.

### AI Skills

- Using NL-to-SQL and AI insight tools responsibly.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI/Tableau/Looker
- SQL
- Excel

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Power BI/Tableau certs
- AIBOS BI certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: SQL, one BI tool, viz basics.
2. Intermediate: data storytelling, KPI design, stats.
3. Advanced: self-serve BI and AI-assisted analytics.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Dashboard adoption
- Insight turnaround
- Decision impact
- Data-accuracy

## Typical Deliverables

- Dashboards
- Reports
- KPI definitions

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Analytics Engineer, Product Manager, Executives.

## Career Progression

1. **Junior Analyst**
2. **BI Analyst**
3. **Senior**
4. **Analytics Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you design a dashboard for executives?
2. How do you ensure a report is accurate?
3. Tell me about an insight that changed a decision.
