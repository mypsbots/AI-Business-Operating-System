## Role Summary

**Mission:** Maximise developer productivity and safety through reusable platform capabilities.

Builds the internal developer platform — the paved 'golden paths', tooling and self-service infrastructure that let other engineers ship quickly and safely.

## Key Responsibilities

- Build self-service developer platforms and templates.
- Create golden-path CI/CD and IaC modules.
- Provide internal tooling, portals and standards.
- Embed security and compliance into the platform.
- Support and improve developer experience.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Pulumi).
- Kubernetes and containers.
- CI/CD pipelines and developer portals (Backstage).
- Cloud platform engineering and golden paths.

### Business Skills

- Treating the platform as an internal product.

### AI Skills

- Embedding AI tooling and agent runtimes into the platform.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Terraform
- Kubernetes
- Backstage
- GitHub Actions
- Helm
- ArgoCD

## Certifications and Qualifications

- CKA/CKAD
- Terraform Associate
- AIBOS Platform Engineering certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: cloud, containers, IaC basics.
2. Intermediate: Kubernetes, CI/CD, platform patterns.
3. Advanced: internal developer platforms and golden paths.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Deployment frequency
- Lead time for changes
- Self-service adoption
- Change-failure rate

## Typical Deliverables

- Platform modules
- Golden-path templates
- Developer portal
- Platform docs

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: DevOps Engineer, SRE, Cloud Engineer, Security.

## Career Progression

1. **DevOps Engineer**
2. **Platform Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **Platform Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What makes a good golden path?
2. How do you balance developer freedom with guardrails?
3. How would you provide self-service environments?
