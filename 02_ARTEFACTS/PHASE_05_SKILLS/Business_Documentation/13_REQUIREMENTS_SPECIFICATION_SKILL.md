## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Requirements Specification captures what a system or automation must do (functional) and how well (non-functional), forming the basis for design, build, testing and acceptance.

Clear requirements reduce rework and anchor the SOW's acceptance criteria.

## When to Use

- Development, automation or integration projects.
- Whenever acceptance criteria depend on specified behaviour.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Functional requirements** — What the system must do, as testable statements.
2. **Non-functional requirements** — Performance, scalability, availability, usability.
3. **Integrations** — Systems/APIs to integrate with.
4. **Environments** — Dev/test/prod and access.
5. **Data inputs and outputs** — Data sources, formats, fields.
6. **Roles and permissions** — Who can do what.
7. **Performance requirements** — Throughput, latency, volumes.
8. **Security requirements** — Authentication, encryption, controls.
9. **Testing requirements** — Test types and coverage.
10. **Regulatory constraints** — Compliance requirements affecting the build.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Discovery notes and stakeholder needs.
- Integration, environment and data details.
- Performance, security, testing and regulatory constraints.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Make each requirement testable so it maps to SOW acceptance criteria.
- Capture data-protection and security requirements early.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Number requirements for traceability to tests.
- Separate must-have from nice-to-have.
