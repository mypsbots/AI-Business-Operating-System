## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Data Sharing Agreement governs situations where both parties independently decide why and how personal data is used (controller-to-controller), setting out purposes, lawful bases, security and responsibilities — which a processor DPA does not cover.

It aligns with the ICO's data sharing code of practice.

## When to Use

- Both parties act as independent (or joint) controllers.
- Sharing personal data for each party's own purposes.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parties and roles** — Each party as controller; joint vs independent.
2. **Purpose of sharing** — Why data is shared and the lawful basis.
3. **Data shared** — Categories of personal data and data subjects.
4. **Method and frequency** — How and how often data is shared.
5. **Security measures** — Protection in transit and at rest.
6. **Data-subject rights and transparency** — How rights and privacy information are handled.
7. **Retention and deletion** — How long each party keeps data.
8. **International transfers** — Mechanism where relevant.
9. **Breach handling** — Notification and cooperation.
10. **Liability and indemnity** — Allocation between controllers.
11. **Governing law** — England and Wales.
12. **Signatures** — Authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Purpose, lawful basis, and data categories.
- Security, retention and transfer details.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Use this instead of a processor DPA when both parties determine purposes and means.
- Follow the ICO data sharing code of practice; confirm a lawful basis for each party.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Be explicit about who is controller for what.
- Provide clear privacy information to data subjects.
