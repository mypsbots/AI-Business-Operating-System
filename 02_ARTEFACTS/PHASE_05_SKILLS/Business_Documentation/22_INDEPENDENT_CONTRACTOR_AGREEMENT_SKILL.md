## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

An Independent Contractor Agreement engages an individual on a self-employed basis, defining deliverables, fees, IP assignment and confidentiality while avoiding the creation of an employment relationship.

It must address UK employment-status and tax considerations (including IR35/off-payroll where relevant).

## When to Use

- Engaging an individual freelancer/contractor.
- Where IP must vest in the Supplier and confidentiality is needed.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parties and services** — Contractor, Supplier, and the services.
2. **Status and independence** — Self-employed; not an employee; substitution where genuine.
3. **Fees and invoicing** — Rates, invoicing, expenses.
4. **Intellectual property assignment** — Present assignment of IP in deliverables to the Supplier.
5. **Confidentiality** — Protection of Supplier and customer information.
6. **Data protection** — Compliance where personal data is handled.
7. **Tax and IR35** — Contractor responsible for own tax; off-payroll status where applicable.
8. **Warranties and liability** — Skill and care; right to enter.
9. **Term and termination** — Duration and notice.
10. **Restrictive covenants** — Reasonable, where justified.
11. **Governing law** — England and Wales.
12. **Signatures** — Signatory and date.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Contractor details and services; fees.
- IP and confidentiality requirements; IR35 status determination.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Assess employment status and IR35/off-payroll rules; misclassification carries tax and employment risk.
- IP does not pass automatically — include a written, signed assignment.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Include a present assignment of IP, effective on creation and payment.
- Keep genuine independence (control, substitution) consistent with contractor status.
