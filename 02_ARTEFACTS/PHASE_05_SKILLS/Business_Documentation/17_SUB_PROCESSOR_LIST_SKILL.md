## At a Glance

The maintained register of approved sub-processors disclosed to the customer under the DPA.

## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Sub-processor List discloses the third parties the Supplier uses to process personal data on the customer's behalf, supporting UK GDPR transparency and the DPA's sub-processor authorisation terms.

Keeping it current is a compliance obligation, not an optional courtesy.

## When to Use

- Whenever a DPA is in place and sub-processors are used.
- When adding, changing or removing a sub-processor.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Sub-processor name** — Legal entity.
2. **Service provided** — What they do for the Supplier.
3. **Personal data processed** — Categories of data they handle.
4. **Location(s)** — Country/region of processing.
5. **Transfer mechanism** — Where relevant (IDTA / UK Addendum).
6. **Safeguards** — Security/compliance status (e.g. ISO 27001).
7. **Effective date and change log** — When added/changed; notice given.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Approved vendor/sub-processor register.
- Processing locations and transfer mechanisms.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Provide advance notice of sub-processor changes so the controller can object.
- Ensure each sub-processor is bound by equivalent data-protection terms.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Keep a single source of truth and version it.
- Notify the controller before onboarding a new sub-processor.
