## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Subcontractor Agreement lets the Supplier engage a specialist to deliver part of a customer project while remaining responsible to the customer. It flows down confidentiality, data-protection, IP and security obligations so the Supplier can meet its customer commitments.

It protects the Supplier by ensuring the subcontractor's terms are compatible with the head contract.

## When to Use

- Engaging specialists, contractors or partners on a customer project.
- Whenever obligations from the MSA/SOW must flow down.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parties and scope** — Supplier, subcontractor, and the subcontracted scope.
2. **Relationship to head contract** — Back-to-back obligations with the customer agreement.
3. **Fees and payment** — Charges and payment terms.
4. **Confidentiality** — Equivalent to the head NDA/MSA.
5. **Data protection (sub-processing)** — UK GDPR flow-down; Supplier authorisation as controller-processor chain.
6. **Intellectual property** — Assignment/licence so the Supplier can grant customer rights.
7. **Security obligations** — Flow-down of the Security Schedule.
8. **Warranties and liability** — Skill and care; liability aligned to head contract.
9. **Insurance** — Required cover.
10. **Term and termination** — Duration and exit.
11. **Governing law** — England and Wales.
12. **Signatures** — Authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Subcontracted scope and fees.
- Head-contract obligations to flow down (IP, data, security, confidentiality).

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- You cannot grant the customer greater IP rights than you obtain from the subcontractor — ensure IP assignment/licence flows through.
- The Supplier remains responsible to the customer for subcontracted work.
- Confirm the subcontractor is an approved sub-processor where personal data is involved.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Keep terms back-to-back with the customer agreement.
- Maintain an internal register of approved subcontractors.
