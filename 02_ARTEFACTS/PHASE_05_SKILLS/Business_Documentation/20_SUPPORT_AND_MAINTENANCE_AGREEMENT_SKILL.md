## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Support and Maintenance Agreement covers what happens after delivery: bug fixes, updates, support and maintenance, with commercial terms for the recurring relationship. It usually incorporates an SLA for response/resolution.

It converts a delivered project into an ongoing, revenue-generating service.

## When to Use

- After go-live, for ongoing support/maintenance.
- For subscription or retainer services.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Scope of support and maintenance** — What is covered (fixes, updates, patches).
2. **Exclusions** — New features, third-party faults, misuse.
3. **Service levels** — Reference the SLA (priorities, response/resolution).
4. **Fees and term** — Recurring fees, term, renewal, uplift.
5. **Change and enhancement requests** — How new work is handled (Change Request).
6. **Customer responsibilities** — Access, environments, cooperation.
7. **Data protection and security** — DPA/Security Schedule references.
8. **Termination and exit** — Notice, exit assistance, data return.
9. **Governing law** — England and Wales.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Support scope, exclusions and SLA.
- Recurring fees, term and renewal.
- DPA/Security references.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Bill recurring services in advance; state renewal and price-review mechanics.
- Reference the SLA rather than duplicating service levels.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Separate support (covered) from enhancements (Change Requests).
- Attach the SLA and keep it current.
