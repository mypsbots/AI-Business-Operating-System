## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Project Closure Report records what was delivered against the SOW, confirms acceptance, lists any outstanding items, captures lessons learned, and documents handover — providing a clean, auditable end to the engagement.

It pairs with the Acceptance Certificate to close delivery and open support/renewal.

## When to Use

- At the end of a project or major phase.
- To transition into support, renewal or closure.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Project summary** — Objectives and what was delivered.
2. **Deliverables vs SOW** — Mapping of delivered items to scope.
3. **Acceptance status** — Reference to the Acceptance Certificate.
4. **Outstanding items** — Snags and follow-ups with owners/dates.
5. **Budget and schedule outcome** — Planned vs actual.
6. **Lessons learned** — What to repeat/improve.
7. **Handover and documentation** — What was handed over.
8. **Support and renewal** — Warranty/support start; renewal options.
9. **Sign-off** — Supplier and customer acknowledgement.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- SOW, Acceptance Certificate, project records.
- Budget/schedule actuals and lessons.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Confirm data return/deletion obligations are addressed at closure.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Capture lessons while fresh.
- Confirm warranty start and support/renewal path.
