## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

The Statement of Work (SOW) describes a single project in precise, measurable terms: scope, deliverables, timeline, price, dependencies and acceptance. It is the document that most often prevents disputes.

It sits under the MSA and should define objective acceptance criteria rather than vague quality statements.

## When to Use

- Every discrete project delivered under an MSA.
- Whenever deliverables, milestones and acceptance need to be fixed.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **SOW reference and effective date** — Unique SOW number; effective date; related MSA reference.
2. **Background and objectives** — Why the project exists and the outcomes sought.
3. **Detailed scope** — Exactly what is in scope.
4. **Excluded items** — Explicitly out of scope.
5. **Deliverables** — Each tangible output and its format.
6. **Milestones and project plan** — Phased plan with dates and owners.
7. **Customer dependencies and materials** — What the customer provides and by when.
8. **Delivery method and location** — On-site/remote; environments.
9. **Personnel and roles** — Key roles on both sides.
10. **Assumptions** — The basis of estimates.
11. **Fees, expenses and payment milestones** — Charges tied to milestones; expenses treatment.
12. **Acceptance criteria** — Objective, measurable criteria per deliverable.
13. **Testing process and acceptance period** — How acceptance is tested and the review window.
14. **Deemed acceptance rule** — Acceptance is deemed if no defects raised within the period.
15. **Support or warranty period** — Post-acceptance warranty/support.
16. **Intellectual-property treatment** — Ownership/licence for this project's deliverables.
17. **Data-processing details** — Whether personal data is processed (trigger DPA schedule).
18. **Special security requirements** — Any project-specific security (trigger Security Schedule).
19. **Signatories** — Authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Related MSA reference; SOW number; dates.
- Scope, exclusions, deliverables, milestones, dependencies.
- Fees and payment milestones; acceptance criteria and testing.
- IP treatment; data-processing and security flags.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Replace vague phrases like 'the system will work well' with measurable criteria (e.g. 'processes the approved test file and creates the specified output fields with no Severity 1 defects').
- For AI work, define performance metrics here rather than assuming them.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Make acceptance criteria objective and testable.
- Tie payments to milestones and acceptance.
- Flag DPA/Security/AI schedules when triggered.
