## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Customer Due-Diligence Checklist verifies the customer's identity, standing and risk before accepting work — covering legal identity, creditworthiness, sanctions, data legality and conflicts.

It protects the Supplier from legal, financial, reputational and regulatory exposure.

## When to Use

- Before accepting any new customer or higher-risk engagement.
- Before signing the MSA/SOW.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Legal identity** — Legal name, company number, Companies House record.
2. **Addresses** — Registered office and trading address.
3. **VAT and tax** — VAT number where relevant.
4. **Authorised signatory** — Who can bind the customer.
5. **Creditworthiness** — Ability to pay / credit check.
6. **Sanctions and PEP screening** — Sanctions exposure.
7. **Adverse media** — For higher-risk work.
8. **Data source and use** — Source and intended use of data.
9. **Regulated-sector requirements** — Sector-specific obligations.
10. **Project legality** — Is the work lawful?
11. **Conflicts of interest** — Any conflicts to manage.
12. **Decision and approver** — Outcome and who approved it.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Customer-provided details; public registers.
- Internal approval matrix thresholds.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Check the Companies House record for UK companies and screen for sanctions.
- Escalate to director approval per the internal matrix for higher-risk indicators.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Complete before signing, not after.
- Record the decision and approver for audit.
