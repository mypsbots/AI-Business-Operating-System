## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Data Mapping Questionnaire establishes whether and how personal data is processed, feeding the DPA processing schedule and any DPIA, and confirming whether the Supplier is a processor or joint/independent controller.

It prevents processing personal data without the correct data terms in place.

## When to Use

- At scoping, whenever personal data may be involved.
- Before finalising the DPA schedule.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Processing activities** — What processing will occur.
2. **Categories of personal data** — Including any special-category data.
3. **Categories of data subjects** — Whose data is involved.
4. **Purpose and lawful basis** — Why data is processed and the basis.
5. **Data sources and flows** — Where data comes from and goes.
6. **Controller/processor determination** — Roles of each party.
7. **Sub-processors and locations** — Who else processes and where.
8. **International transfers** — Any restricted transfers and mechanism.
9. **Retention** — How long data is kept.
10. **Risk indicators** — Triggers for a DPIA.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Scope and data flows from discovery.
- Customer information on data categories and subjects.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Special-category data, large-scale or high-risk processing may require a DPIA.
- Use the output to complete the DPA processing schedule accurately.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Complete before any personal data is shared.
- Escalate high-risk findings to the DPO/solicitor.
