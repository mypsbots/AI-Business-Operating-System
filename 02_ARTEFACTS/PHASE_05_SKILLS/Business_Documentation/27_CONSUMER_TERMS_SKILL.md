## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

Consumer Terms apply where the customer is an individual acting mainly outside their trade or profession. UK consumer law adds mandatory protections — fairness, transparency and cancellation rights for distance contracts — that B2B terms do not address.

They must be fair and transparent; unfair terms are unenforceable.

## When to Use

- Selling bespoke services directly to individuals (consumers).
- Distance/online contracts with consumers.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Who we are and how to contact us** — Trader identity and contact.
2. **Services and price** — Clear description and total price (incl. VAT).
3. **Ordering and acceptance** — How the contract forms.
4. **Right to cancel** — 14-day cancellation for distance contracts and exceptions.
5. **Performance and timing** — When services are provided; consumer consent to start early.
6. **Consumer rights** — Statutory rights (reasonable care and skill; remedies).
7. **Payment** — Amounts and timing.
8. **Liability** — Fair terms; statutory rights not excluded.
9. **Complaints and dispute resolution** — How to complain; ADR/ODR information.
10. **Data protection** — Reference the Privacy Notice.
11. **Governing law** — England and Wales (consumer's statutory protections preserved).

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Service description and total price (incl. VAT).
- Cancellation and performance-start details.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Consumer Rights Act 2015 and Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 apply; terms must be fair and transparent.
- Distance contracts carry a 14-day right to cancel (with limited exceptions); handle early-start consent correctly.
- Obtain legal advice before selling bespoke services directly to consumers.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Never reuse B2B terms for consumers unchanged.
- Make cancellation rights and total price prominent.
