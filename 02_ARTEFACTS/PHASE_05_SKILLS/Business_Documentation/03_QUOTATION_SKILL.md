## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Quotation is a short, priced offer for a clearly defined scope, used where a full Proposal is unnecessary. It states the price, what it covers, and how long it is valid.

Like a Proposal, it can bind on acceptance, so its status and validity must be explicit.

## When to Use

- Small or well-defined work where a full Proposal is not needed.
- Responding quickly to a customer request for price.
- Fixed, catalogue-style services with known pricing.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Quotation reference and date** — Unique quote number and issue date.
2. **Supplier and Customer details** — Legal names and contacts.
3. **Scope of the quoted work** — Precisely what is included.
4. **Exclusions and assumptions** — What is not included; the basis of the price.
5. **Price and VAT** — Net price, VAT position, total.
6. **Payment terms** — Deposit and payment days.
7. **Validity period** — How long the quoted price stands (e.g. 14–30 days).
8. **Acceptance mechanism** — How the customer accepts (signature / PO / written confirmation).
9. **Reference to governing terms** — The T&Cs / MSA that apply on acceptance.
10. **Status marking** — 'Subject to contract' unless intended to bind on acceptance.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Defined scope and exclusions.
- Price basis, VAT position, payment terms, validity period.
- Governing terms reference and acceptance method.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- A quotation is generally treated as an offer capable of acceptance — keep scope tight and validity explicit.
- State which terms govern once accepted, to avoid a 'battle of the forms' with a customer PO.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Use for defined work only; use a Proposal for anything with material assumptions.
- Always include a validity period and governing terms.
