## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Project Plan organises delivery: what will be done, by whom, by when, and how progress and decisions are managed. It keeps larger engagements on track and transparent.

It complements the SOW milestones with operational detail.

## When to Use

- Larger or multi-phase projects.
- Where dependencies and decision deadlines must be tracked.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Work breakdown** — Phases, workstreams and tasks.
2. **Milestones** — Key dates tied to SOW payments/acceptance.
3. **Owners** — Responsible person per task/workstream.
4. **Dependencies** — Internal and customer dependencies.
5. **Risks** — Key risks and mitigations (link to Risk Register).
6. **Communication cadence** — Meetings, status updates.
7. **Reporting** — What is reported and to whom.
8. **Decision deadlines** — Dates by which customer decisions are needed.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- SOW scope, deliverables and milestones.
- Team and roles; customer dependencies.
- Reporting cadence and decision deadlines.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Customer delay should entitle the Supplier to adjust the timeline (and, where appropriate, fees) — reflect this in the SOW.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Tie milestones to SOW payments and acceptance.
- Make customer decision deadlines explicit.
