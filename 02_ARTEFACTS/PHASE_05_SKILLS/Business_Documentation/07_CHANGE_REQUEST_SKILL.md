## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Change Request captures any material change to an in-flight project so that scope, price, timeline and acceptance stay controlled. No material change should be accepted only via a call or informal message.

It protects both parties by documenting the impact of the change and the authorisation to proceed.

## When to Use

- Any change to scope, deliverables, price, timeline or resources.
- When customer requests would alter acceptance criteria.
- Whenever the impact must be agreed before work continues.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Change Request reference** — Unique CR number; related SOW/MSA; date.
2. **Requested change** — Precisely what is to change.
3. **Reason** — Why the change is needed.
4. **Effect on scope** — Additions/removals to scope and deliverables.
5. **Effect on price** — Revised fees and payment impact.
6. **Effect on timeline** — Revised dates/milestones.
7. **Effect on resources** — People/skills impact.
8. **Technical or security consequences** — Any risk or architecture impact.
9. **Revised acceptance criteria** — Updated measurable criteria.
10. **Authorisation clause** — Supplier is not required to start changed work until the CR is approved.
11. **Signatures** — Both parties' authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Related SOW/MSA reference; CR number.
- The change, its reason, and impacts on scope/price/timeline/resources.
- Revised acceptance criteria.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- State expressly that changed work does not start until the CR is approved.
- A signed Change Request typically ranks above the SOW in the order of precedence.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Never begin changed work on a verbal request.
- Quantify price and timeline impact precisely.
