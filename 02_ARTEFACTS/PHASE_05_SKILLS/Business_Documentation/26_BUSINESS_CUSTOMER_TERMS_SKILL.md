## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

Business Customer Terms and Conditions provide standard B2B contract terms for straightforward engagements without a full negotiated MSA. For a small, low-risk project they can be combined with an SOW into a single Services Agreement.

They compress the essential MSA protections into a reusable, acceptance-based form.

## When to Use

- Small or standardised B2B engagements.
- As the combined Services Agreement + SOW for very small projects.
- Where a full negotiated MSA is disproportionate.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Scope and services** — What is provided and exclusions.
2. **Deliverables and acceptance** — Outputs and acceptance criteria.
3. **Timeline** — Indicative delivery.
4. **Fees and payment** — Charges, deposit, payment days, late payment.
5. **Change control** — How changes are agreed.
6. **Intellectual property** — Background IP vs deliverables; assignment on payment.
7. **Confidentiality** — Mutual obligations.
8. **Data protection** — UK GDPR; DPA where personal data is processed.
9. **Warranties and AI limitations** — Skill and care; AI output caveats.
10. **Liability** — Cap and excluded losses; non-excludable liabilities preserved.
11. **Termination** — Duration and exit.
12. **Governing law** — England and Wales; exclusive jurisdiction.
13. **Acceptance and signatures** — How the customer accepts.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Standard firm positions (payment, IP, liability).
- Scope, deliverables and acceptance for the specific engagement.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- State which document prevails if a customer PO conflicts with these terms.
- Preserve non-excludable liabilities; align the cap to insurance.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Use for small/standard work; use MSA+SOW for complex engagements.
- Keep consistent with the MSA so they can scale up cleanly.
