## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Website Cookie Notice discloses the cookies and similar technologies in use and supports a compliant consent mechanism (consent required for non-essential cookies under PECR, with UK GDPR-standard consent).

It must reflect an actual cookie audit and allow users to accept, reject and manage cookies.

## When to Use

- Any website using cookies or similar tracking.
- Whenever analytics, advertising or embedded third-party content is used.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **What cookies are** — Brief explanation.
2. **Cookies we use** — Table: name, provider, purpose, type, duration.
3. **Strictly necessary vs optional** — Distinguish essential from non-essential.
4. **Consent mechanism** — How consent is obtained, refused and withdrawn.
5. **Third-party cookies** — Analytics, advertising, embeds.
6. **Managing preferences** — How to change settings/browser controls.
7. **Links** — Reference the Privacy Notice.
8. **Updates** — How changes are handled.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Cookie audit: each cookie's name, provider, purpose, duration.
- Consent tool/mechanism details.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- PECR requires prior consent for non-essential cookies; essential cookies are exempt.
- Consent must be as easy to refuse as to give — no pre-ticked boxes.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Base the notice on a real, current cookie audit.
- Provide granular accept/reject controls.
