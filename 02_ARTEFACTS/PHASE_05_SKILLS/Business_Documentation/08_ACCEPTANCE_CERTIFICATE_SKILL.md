## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

An Acceptance (or Completion) Certificate provides written confirmation that the customer has accepted the deliverables, closing the acceptance process defined in the SOW and starting the warranty/support period.

It removes ambiguity about whether the work is done and unlocks the final invoice.

## When to Use

- At completion of a project or milestone with acceptance criteria.
- Before issuing the final invoice.
- To start the warranty/support clock.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Reference and parties** — Related SOW/MSA; parties; date.
2. **Deliverables received** — List of accepted deliverables.
3. **Testing completed** — Confirmation acceptance testing was performed.
4. **Outstanding minor items** — Snag list, if any, with target dates.
5. **Effective acceptance date** — The date acceptance takes effect.
6. **Start of warranty or support** — When the warranty/support period begins.
7. **Approval to issue final invoice** — Authorisation to invoice the balance.
8. **Signatures** — Customer acceptance; Supplier countersignature.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Related SOW; list of deliverables and test results.
- Any outstanding minor items and dates.
- Acceptance date and warranty start.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Tie acceptance to the SOW's objective criteria and any deemed-acceptance rule.
- Record snags separately so acceptance is not blocked by trivial items.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Obtain written acceptance before final invoicing.
- Keep the signed certificate in the customer's closure records.
