## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** business document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. For high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive or complex engagements, obtain solicitor sign-off before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Website Privacy Notice tells visitors what personal data the business collects, why, the lawful basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and their rights — satisfying UK GDPR Articles 13–14 transparency duties.

It must be accurate to actual processing and kept up to date.

## When to Use

- Any public website or app collecting personal data.
- Whenever processing purposes or third parties change.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. Keep numbering and the order of precedence consistent with the [UK Contracting Framework](00_UK_CONTRACTING_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Who we are** — Controller identity and contact (and DPO if any).
2. **What data we collect** — Categories of personal data.
3. **How we collect it** — Direct, automated, third-party sources.
4. **Purposes and lawful bases** — Why, and the UK GDPR lawful basis for each.
5. **Cookies and tracking** — Reference the Cookie Notice.
6. **Sharing and processors** — Who data is shared with.
7. **International transfers** — Any transfers and safeguards.
8. **Retention** — How long data is kept.
9. **Your rights** — Access, rectification, erasure, objection, portability, complaint to the ICO.
10. **Security** — How data is protected.
11. **Changes and contact** — How updates are notified; contact details.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Data inventory: what is collected, why, lawful bases.
- Processors/third parties, transfers, retention periods.
- Controller and (if any) DPO contact details.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Must meet UK GDPR Articles 13–14; include the right to complain to the ICO.
- Keep it specific to actual processing — generic notices are non-compliant.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Layer the notice for readability (summary + detail).
- Review whenever processing changes.
