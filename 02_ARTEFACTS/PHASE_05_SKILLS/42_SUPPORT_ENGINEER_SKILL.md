## Role Summary

**Mission:** Resolve issues fast and turn problems into improvements.

Resolves customer technical issues quickly and empathetically, and feeds recurring problems back into the product and knowledge base.

## Key Responsibilities

- Diagnose and resolve customer technical issues.
- Triage and escalate complex problems.
- Write and maintain knowledge-base articles.
- Meet SLAs and keep customers informed.
- Surface recurring issues to product/engineering.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Product internals, logs and troubleshooting.
- APIs, basic SQL and debugging.
- Ticketing and reproduction skills.

### Business Skills

- Customer empathy and communication.

### AI Skills

- Using AI assistants for triage and drafting; maintaining KB for RAG.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Zendesk/Intercom
- Logging tools
- Status pages
- KB tools

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Support certs
- AIBOS Support certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: product, ticketing, troubleshooting.
2. Intermediate: debugging, APIs, KB writing.
3. Advanced: technical escalation and AI-assisted support.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- First-response/resolution time
- CSAT
- Deflection rate
- Reopen rate

## Typical Deliverables

- Resolved tickets
- KB articles
- Issue trends

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Customer Success Manager, Backend Engineer, Knowledge Manager.

## Career Progression

1. **Support Engineer**
2. **Senior**
3. **Support Lead / TAM**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. Walk through how you debug a customer issue.
2. How do you handle an angry customer under SLA pressure?
3. How do you write a good KB article?
