## Role Summary

**Mission:** Protect the organisation legally while enabling the business.

Provides legal advice and manages legal risk — contracts, regulation, IP and disputes — and increasingly AI/data governance.

## Key Responsibilities

- Draft, review and negotiate contracts.
- Advise on regulation, IP and data/AI law.
- Manage legal risk and disputes.
- Support privacy and compliance programmes.
- Maintain policies and templates.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Legal research tools and contract systems.

### Business Skills

- Contract law, IP and commercial negotiation.
- Regulatory landscape (privacy, AI, sector rules).
- Risk assessment and pragmatic advice.

### AI Skills

- AI/data regulation (EU AI Act, GDPR) and AI use in legal work.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- CLM (Ironclad)
- Legal research (Westlaw)
- DocuSign

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Qualified lawyer (jurisdiction)
- AIBOS AI Law certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: contract and commercial law.
2. Intermediate: IP, privacy, regulatory.
3. Advanced: AI law and governance.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Contract turnaround
- Legal risk reduction
- Compliance posture

## Typical Deliverables

- Contracts
- Legal advice
- Policies

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Compliance Specialist, Privacy Engineer, Executive Leadership.

## Career Progression

1. **Associate**
2. **Counsel**
3. **Senior Counsel**
4. **General Counsel**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you assess risk in a vendor contract?
2. How does the EU AI Act affect our products?
3. How do you balance legal caution with business speed?
