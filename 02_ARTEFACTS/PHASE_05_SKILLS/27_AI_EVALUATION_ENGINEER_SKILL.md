## Role Summary

**Mission:** Make AI quality measurable, comparable and continuously monitored.

Measures whether AI systems actually work — building evaluation datasets, metrics and harnesses to quantify quality, safety and regressions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Build evaluation datasets and golden sets.
- Define metrics (accuracy, faithfulness, safety, cost).
- Implement automated eval harnesses and CI gates.
- Detect regressions across model/prompt changes.
- Report quality trends to stakeholders.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Eval design, LLM-as-judge, human eval.
- Metrics for RAG, agents and generation.
- Statistics, benchmarking and CI integration.

### Business Skills

- Tying eval metrics to product outcomes.

### AI Skills

- Deep understanding of failure modes and safety evals.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Ragas/Promptfoo
- LangSmith/Braintrust
- Weights & Biases
- pytest

## Certifications and Qualifications

- AIBOS AI Evaluation certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: metrics, prompting, statistics.
2. Intermediate: eval harnesses, LLM-as-judge, RAG/agent evals.
3. Advanced: safety evals and continuous evaluation systems.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Eval coverage
- Regression-catch rate
- Correlation of evals with outcomes

## Typical Deliverables

- Eval datasets
- Eval harnesses
- Quality dashboards

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: LLM/RAG/Agent Engineers, AI Safety Engineer, QA.

## Career Progression

1. **ML/QA Engineer**
2. **AI Evaluation Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **AI Quality Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you build an eval set for a new AI feature?
2. When is LLM-as-judge appropriate and risky?
3. How do you catch quality regressions in CI?
