## Role Summary

**Mission:** Win deals by proving technical fit and value to buyers.

The technical expert in the sales process — runs demos, answers technical questions, scopes solutions and removes technical blockers to closing deals.

## Key Responsibilities

- Run technical demos and proofs of concept.
- Scope solutions to customer requirements.
- Answer technical/security questions and RFPs.
- Support trials and remove technical blockers.
- Feed customer needs back to product.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- The product's architecture and integrations.
- Solution scoping and POC building.
- Security/compliance Q&A.

### Business Skills

- Linking technical capability to business value.

### AI Skills

- Demoing AI features and explaining AI value/risk to buyers.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- CRM (Salesforce)
- Demo environments
- Slide tools
- POC sandboxes

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Product/cloud certs
- AIBOS Sales Engineering certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: the product, demo skills, discovery.
2. Intermediate: solution scoping, POCs, objection handling.
3. Advanced: enterprise architecture selling and AI value.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Technical win rate
- POC success rate
- Sales-cycle time
- Influenced revenue

## Typical Deliverables

- Demos
- POCs
- Solution proposals
- RFP responses

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Account Manager, Product Manager, Solution Architect.

## Career Progression

1. **Support/Engineer**
2. **Sales Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **SE Manager**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you tailor a demo to a prospect?
2. How do you handle a tough technical objection?
3. How do you scope a POC for success?
