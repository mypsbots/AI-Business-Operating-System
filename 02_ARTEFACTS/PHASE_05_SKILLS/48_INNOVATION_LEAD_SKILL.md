## Role Summary

**Mission:** Find and validate the next sources of growth and advantage.

Drives the organisation's innovation agenda — spotting emerging opportunities, running experiments and incubating new products or business models.

## Key Responsibilities

- Scan trends and emerging technology.
- Run innovation programmes and experiments.
- Incubate and validate new concepts.
- Build business cases for bets.
- Foster an innovation culture.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Enough tech literacy to assess feasibility.

### Business Skills

- Opportunity assessment and business-model design.
- Lean experimentation and venture building.
- Stakeholder buy-in and portfolio management.

### AI Skills

- Spotting and validating AI-driven opportunities.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Innovation/portfolio tools
- Prototyping tools
- Trend sources

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Design thinking / Lean Startup
- AIBOS Innovation certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: design thinking, lean startup.
2. Intermediate: opportunity assessment, experimentation.
3. Advanced: venture building and innovation strategy.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Validated opportunities
- Experiments run
- Pipeline value
- Concepts graduated

## Typical Deliverables

- Opportunity assessments
- Prototypes
- Business cases

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Research Scientist, Product Manager, Executive Leadership.

## Career Progression

1. **Product/Strategy role**
2. **Innovation Lead**
3. **Head of Innovation**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you evaluate a new opportunity?
2. How do you run a fast, cheap validation experiment?
3. How do you build support for a risky bet?
