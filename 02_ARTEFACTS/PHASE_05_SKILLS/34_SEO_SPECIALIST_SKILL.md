## Role Summary

**Mission:** Grow qualified organic traffic and rankings sustainably.

Improves a site's visibility in traditional search engines (Google, Bing) so it attracts more organic traffic.

## Key Responsibilities

- Keyword research and content strategy.
- On-page, technical and off-page SEO.
- Site audits (speed, crawlability, structured data).
- Link building and content optimisation.
- Track rankings, traffic and conversions.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Technical SEO (crawling, indexing, Core Web Vitals).
- Structured data (schema.org), sitemaps.
- Analytics and Search Console.

### Business Skills

- Aligning SEO to demand and revenue.

### AI Skills

- Using AI for content/keyword work; understanding AI search shift.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Google Search Console
- Ahrefs/Semrush
- Screaming Frog
- GA4

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Google Analytics
- AIBOS SEO certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: how search works, keywords, on-page.
2. Intermediate: technical SEO, link building, analytics.
3. Advanced: enterprise SEO and AI-search adaptation.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Organic traffic
- Keyword rankings
- Organic conversions
- Indexation health

## Typical Deliverables

- SEO strategy
- Audits
- Optimised content

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Content Strategist, Frontend Engineer, Growth Marketer.

## Career Progression

1. **SEO Specialist**
2. **Senior**
3. **SEO Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you audit a site for technical SEO?
2. How is SEO changing with AI search?
3. How do you prioritise SEO work?
