## Role Summary

**Mission:** Ensure systems meet reliability targets while enabling change.

Keeps production systems reliable using engineering and automation — owning SLOs, monitoring, incident response and toil reduction.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and track SLOs, SLIs and error budgets.
- Build monitoring, alerting and dashboards.
- Lead incident response and post-mortems.
- Automate toil and improve resilience.
- Plan capacity and run chaos/DR exercises.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Observability (metrics, logs, traces; OpenTelemetry).
- SLO/error-budget engineering.
- Kubernetes, automation and scripting.
- Incident management and resilience patterns.

### Business Skills

- Balancing reliability against feature velocity.

### AI Skills

- Using AIOps for anomaly detection and incident triage.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Prometheus/Grafana
- Datadog
- PagerDuty
- Kubernetes
- OpenTelemetry

## Certifications and Qualifications

- CKA
- Cloud certs
- AIBOS SRE certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: Linux, networking, monitoring.
2. Intermediate: SLOs, incident response, Kubernetes.
3. Advanced: resilience engineering and AIOps.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- SLO attainment
- MTTR
- Toil percentage
- Incident frequency

## Typical Deliverables

- SLOs and dashboards
- Runbooks
- Post-mortems
- Automation

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: DevOps, Backend Engineer, Platform Engineer.

## Career Progression

1. **Engineer**
2. **SRE**
3. **Senior SRE**
4. **SRE Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you set an SLO and error budget?
2. Walk through how you run an incident.
3. How do you reduce toil systematically?
