## Role Summary

**Mission:** Ensure the enterprise's systems and capabilities align to strategy and evolve coherently rather than as silos.

Designs the big-picture technology and capability landscape of the whole enterprise, ensuring systems, data and AI fit a coherent, secure, future-proof strategy.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define target-state architecture and capability maps.
- Set architecture principles, standards and reference patterns.
- Govern major technology and AI platform decisions.
- Manage technical debt and modernisation roadmaps.
- Align architecture to security, compliance and cost goals.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Domain-driven design and capability modelling.
- Integration, data and cloud architecture.
- Architecture frameworks (TOGAF, C4) and ADRs.
- AI/LLM platform architecture patterns.

### Business Skills

- Linking architecture to business capabilities and value.
- Total-cost-of-ownership and build-vs-buy analysis.
- Stakeholder alignment and governance facilitation.

### AI Skills

- Reference architectures for RAG, agents and MCP.
- Governing model selection, data flows and AI risk.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- C4 / Structurizr
- Lucidchart / Miro
- ArchiMate tooling
- Cloud well-architected tools

## Certifications and Qualifications

- TOGAF
- Cloud architect (AWS/Azure/GCP) Pro
- AIBOS AI Architect certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: architecture principles, C4, cloud basics.
2. Intermediate: DDD, integration patterns, TOGAF.
3. Advanced: enterprise AI platform and governance architecture.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Architecture-standard adherence
- Reuse across teams
- Tech-debt reduction
- Time-to-decision on architecture

## Typical Deliverables

- Target-state architecture
- Capability maps
- Architecture standards
- ADRs

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Solution Architect, Security Engineer, Platform Engineer, Executive Leadership.

## Career Progression

1. **Senior Engineer**
2. **Solution Architect**
3. **Enterprise Architect**
4. **Chief Architect**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you align architecture decisions to business strategy?
2. Describe a reference architecture for an enterprise RAG platform.
3. How do you manage technical debt across many teams?
4. How do you govern AI platform choices?
