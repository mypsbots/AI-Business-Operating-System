## Role Summary

**Mission:** Turn user needs and product goals into intuitive, accessible, high-quality experiences.

Designs the end-to-end user experience and interface — from research and flows to polished, accessible, usable screens — so the product is easy and delightful to use.

## Key Responsibilities

- Conduct user research and usability testing.
- Design user flows, wireframes and high-fidelity UI.
- Build and maintain the design system and components.
- Ensure accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and responsive design.
- Prototype and validate concepts with users.
- Partner with engineers to ensure faithful implementation.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Interaction and visual design; design systems and tokens.
- Prototyping and motion design.
- Working knowledge of HTML/CSS and front-end constraints.

### Business Skills

- Translating product goals into design priorities.
- Presenting and defending design decisions to stakeholders.

### AI Skills

- Using AI for ideation, image/variant generation and copy drafts.
- Designing UX patterns for AI features (transparency, undo, trust).

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Figma
- FigJam
- Framer
- Maze / UserTesting
- Storybook
- Design tokens

## Certifications and Qualifications

- NN/g UX Certification
- IDF (Interaction Design Foundation)
- AIBOS Product Design certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: UX principles, Figma, accessibility basics.
2. Intermediate: design systems, research methods, prototyping.
3. Advanced: designing for AI/agentic experiences at scale.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Usability success rate
- Task completion time
- Accessibility conformance
- Design-system adoption

## Typical Deliverables

- Research findings
- Flows and wireframes
- High-fidelity designs
- Design-system components

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Product Manager, Frontend Engineer, Accessibility Specialist, Content Strategist.

## Career Progression

1. **Junior Designer**
2. **Product Designer**
3. **Senior Designer**
4. **Design Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. Walk me through your design process for a recent project.
2. How do you make sure designs are accessible?
3. How would you design a trustworthy AI feature UI?
4. How do you handle conflicting stakeholder feedback?
