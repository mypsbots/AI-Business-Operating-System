## Role Summary

**Mission:** Keep the foundational infrastructure reliable, secure and performant.

Manages the underlying compute, network and storage infrastructure (cloud or hybrid) that everything else runs on.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provision and maintain compute, storage and network.
- Automate configuration and patching.
- Manage capacity, backups and resilience.
- Harden infrastructure and manage access.
- Troubleshoot infrastructure incidents.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Linux, networking and storage fundamentals.
- Configuration management (Ansible) and IaC.
- Virtualisation/containers and monitoring.

### Business Skills

- Capacity and cost planning.

### AI Skills

- Provisioning infrastructure for AI/ML workloads.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Linux
- Ansible
- Terraform
- Prometheus
- VMware/KVM

## Certifications and Qualifications

- RHCSA/RHCE
- Network/cloud certs
- AIBOS Infrastructure certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: Linux, networking, storage.
2. Intermediate: automation, monitoring, hardening.
3. Advanced: hybrid cloud and AI infrastructure.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Infrastructure availability
- Patch compliance
- Capacity headroom
- MTTR

## Typical Deliverables

- Provisioned infrastructure
- Automation playbooks
- Capacity/DR plans

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Cloud Engineer, SRE, Security.

## Career Progression

1. **Sysadmin**
2. **Infrastructure Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **Infra Architect**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you automate OS patching at scale?
2. How do you plan capacity for a growing workload?
3. How do you harden a Linux fleet?
