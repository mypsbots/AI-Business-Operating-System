## Role Summary

**Mission:** Maximise customer and business value by building the right things in the right order.

Owns the 'why' and 'what' of a product: discovers customer problems, defines priorities, and coordinates design, engineering and go-to-market to ship outcomes that move business metrics.

## Key Responsibilities

- Discover and validate customer problems through research and data.
- Own and prioritise the product roadmap and backlog.
- Write clear PRDs, user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Define success metrics and analyse outcomes after launch.
- Coordinate design, engineering, marketing and sales.
- Make scope/trade-off decisions and communicate them.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Reading product analytics and running A/B tests.
- Understanding APIs, data models and system constraints.
- Writing SQL for self-serve analysis.

### Business Skills

- Market and competitor analysis; pricing and packaging basics.
- Prioritisation frameworks (RICE, MoSCoW, Kano).
- Stakeholder management and roadmap communication.
- Unit economics and business-case construction.

### AI Skills

- Designing AI features and evaluating their quality/risk.
- Using AI for research synthesis, PRD drafting and ticket grooming.
- Defining guardrails and human-in-the-loop for AI features.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

- Jira / Linear
- Productboard / Aha!
- Amplitude / Mixpanel

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Pragmatic Institute
- Reforge
- SAFe PO/PM
- AIBOS AI Product Manager certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: product discovery, user stories, roadmapping.
2. Intermediate: experimentation, analytics, pricing.
3. Advanced: AI product strategy, platform/marketplace products.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Adoption / activation rate
- Feature-driven revenue or retention
- Time-to-value
- Roadmap predictability

## Typical Deliverables

- Product roadmap
- PRDs and user stories
- Launch plans
- Outcome reviews

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Product Designer, Engineers, Marketing, Sales, Support.

## Career Progression

1. **Associate PM**
2. **Product Manager**
3. **Senior PM**
4. **Director / VP Product**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you decide what to build next?
2. Walk through how you'd validate a new feature idea.
3. How would you design and measure an AI-powered feature?
4. Tell me about a launch that failed and what you learned.
