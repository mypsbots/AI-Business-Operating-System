## Role Summary

**Mission:** Find and scale repeatable, profitable growth loops.

Drives measurable growth across the funnel through experimentation, channels and optimisation of acquisition, activation and retention.

## Key Responsibilities

- Run growth experiments across channels.
- Optimise acquisition, activation and retention.
- Manage paid, lifecycle and referral programmes.
- Analyse funnels and CAC/LTV.
- Build growth loops and onboarding.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Analytics, funnels and A/B testing.
- Marketing automation and attribution.
- Basic SQL and dashboards.

### Business Skills

- CAC/LTV, unit economics and channel strategy.

### AI Skills

- Using AI for creative, targeting and experiment analysis.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- GA4/Amplitude
- HubSpot/Customer.io
- Ad platforms
- A/B test tools

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Reforge Growth
- AIBOS Growth certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: funnels, analytics, channels.
2. Intermediate: experimentation, lifecycle, attribution.
3. Advanced: growth modelling and AI-driven growth.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- CAC and LTV
- Activation/retention rate
- Experiment win rate
- Funnel conversion

## Typical Deliverables

- Growth experiments
- Funnel analyses
- Lifecycle campaigns

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Product Manager, Content Strategist, Data Scientist.

## Career Progression

1. **Marketer**
2. **Growth Marketer**
3. **Senior**
4. **Head of Growth**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you design a growth experiment?
2. How do you improve activation?
3. How do you balance CAC and LTV?
