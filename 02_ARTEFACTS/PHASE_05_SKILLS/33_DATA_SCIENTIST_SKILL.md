## At a Glance

Uses statistics, machine learning and experimentation to extract insight and build predictive/analytical models that drive decisions.

## Role Summary

**Mission:** Answer hard questions and build models that create measurable value.

Uses statistics, machine learning and experimentation to extract insight and build predictive/analytical models that drive decisions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Frame business problems as data/ML problems.
- Perform EDA, statistical analysis and modelling.
- Design and analyse experiments (A/B tests).
- Communicate findings and recommendations.
- Partner with ML engineers to productionise models.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Statistics, experimentation and ML.
- Python (pandas, scikit-learn), SQL.
- Data visualisation and causal inference.

### Business Skills

- Translating ambiguous questions into analyses.

### AI Skills

- Applying and evaluating GenAI where appropriate.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python/Jupyter
- scikit-learn
- SQL
- Statsmodels

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Cloud/ML certs
- AIBOS Data Science certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: statistics, Python, SQL.
2. Intermediate: ML, experimentation, visualisation.
3. Advanced: causal inference and applied GenAI.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Model/analysis impact
- Experiment velocity
- Prediction accuracy
- Stakeholder adoption

## Typical Deliverables

- Analyses
- Models
- Experiment readouts

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: ML Engineer, Data/Analytics Engineer, Product Manager.

## Career Progression

1. **Analyst**
2. **Data Scientist**
3. **Senior**
4. **Principal / Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How would you design an A/B test for [feature]?
2. How do you avoid misleading conclusions from data?
3. Walk through a model you built end-to-end.
