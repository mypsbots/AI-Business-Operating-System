## Role Summary

**Mission:** Deliver the right content to the right audience to drive outcomes.

Plans and governs content across the customer journey so it is useful, on-brand, discoverable and drives business goals.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define content strategy, pillars and calendar.
- Govern voice, tone and quality standards.
- Plan content for SEO/AEO/GEO discoverability.
- Brief writers and AI tools; edit for quality.
- Measure content performance and iterate.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Content modelling and CMS.
- SEO-aware content structure and metadata.
- Editorial and information architecture.

### Business Skills

- Mapping content to funnel and personas.

### AI Skills

- Using AI to scale content while keeping quality and accuracy.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- CMS (Contentful/WordPress)
- SEO tools
- Editorial calendars
- AI writing tools

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Content marketing certs
- AIBOS Content certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: writing, editing, SEO basics.
2. Intermediate: content strategy, governance, analytics.
3. Advanced: AI-assisted content operations at scale.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Content-driven traffic/leads
- Engagement
- Content velocity
- Quality/accuracy

## Typical Deliverables

- Content strategy
- Editorial calendar
- Style guide

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: SEO/AEO/GEO Specialists, Product Designer, Growth Marketer.

## Career Progression

1. **Writer**
2. **Content Strategist**
3. **Senior**
4. **Head of Content**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you build a content strategy from scratch?
2. How do you keep AI-generated content accurate and on-brand?
3. How do you measure content success?
