## Role Summary

**Mission:** Make the product usable by everyone, regardless of ability.

Ensures products are usable by people with disabilities and meet accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA) and legal requirements.

## Key Responsibilities

- Audit products against WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Test with assistive technologies (screen readers, keyboard).
- Advise design and engineering on accessible patterns.
- Define accessibility acceptance criteria and gates.
- Train teams and track conformance.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- WCAG 2.2, ARIA, semantic HTML.
- Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) and keyboard testing.
- Automated and manual accessibility testing.

### Business Skills

- Accessibility law (ADA, EN 301 549) and risk.

### AI Skills

- Accessibility of AI/chat interfaces and generated content.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- axe
- WAVE
- NVDA/VoiceOver
- Lighthouse

## Certifications and Qualifications

- IAAP CPACC/WAS
- AIBOS Accessibility certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: WCAG principles, semantic HTML, ARIA.
2. Intermediate: assistive-tech testing, remediation.
3. Advanced: accessibility strategy and AI interfaces.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- WCAG conformance level
- Accessibility defects
- Remediation time

## Typical Deliverables

- Accessibility audits
- Remediation guidance
- Acceptance criteria

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Product Designer, Frontend Engineer, QA Engineer.

## Career Progression

1. **Specialist**
2. **Senior Specialist**
3. **Accessibility Lead**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you audit a page for accessibility?
2. What are common WCAG failures and fixes?
3. How do you make a chat UI accessible?
