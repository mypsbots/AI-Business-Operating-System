## Role Summary

**Mission:** Grow and retain revenue by keeping customers successful and loyal.

Owns customer relationships commercially — driving renewals, expansion and satisfaction within assigned accounts.

## Key Responsibilities

- Own renewals, upsell and cross-sell.
- Build relationships with stakeholders.
- Run account plans and QBRs.
- Coordinate internal teams for the customer.
- Forecast and manage the account pipeline.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Understanding the product enough to advise.

### Business Skills

- Negotiation, account planning and forecasting.
- Commercial acumen and value selling.
- Stakeholder management.

### AI Skills

- Using AI for account insights and outreach.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Salesforce/HubSpot
- Gong
- QBR templates

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Sales methodology (MEDDIC)
- AIBOS Account Management certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: product, CRM, account basics.
2. Intermediate: negotiation, account planning, forecasting.
3. Advanced: enterprise account strategy.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Net revenue retention
- Renewal rate
- Expansion revenue
- Forecast accuracy

## Typical Deliverables

- Account plans
- QBRs
- Renewal/expansion deals

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: Sales Engineer, Customer Success Manager, Finance.

## Career Progression

1. **Account Executive**
2. **Account Manager**
3. **Senior**
4. **Sales Director**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you grow an existing account?
2. How do you handle a renewal at risk?
3. How do you forecast accurately?
