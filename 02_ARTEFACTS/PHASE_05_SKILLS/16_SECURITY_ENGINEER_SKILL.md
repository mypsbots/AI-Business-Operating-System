## Role Summary

**Mission:** Reduce security risk across the enterprise to an acceptable level.

Protects the organisation's systems and data from threats by designing controls, hunting vulnerabilities and responding to incidents.

## Key Responsibilities

- Threat modelling and security architecture review.
- Vulnerability management and penetration testing.
- Identity, access and encryption controls.
- Security monitoring and incident response.
- Secure-by-design guidance to engineering teams.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Application, cloud and network security.
- Threat modelling (STRIDE), OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK.
- IAM, cryptography, Zero Trust.
- SIEM/detection and incident response.

### Business Skills

- Risk-based prioritisation and communication.

### AI Skills

- Securing LLM apps (OWASP LLM Top 10), prompt-injection defence.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Burp Suite
- Nessus/Qualys
- SIEM (Splunk/Sentinel)
- Vault
- MITRE ATT&CK

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Security+
- OSCP
- CISSP
- AIBOS AI Security certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: security fundamentals, OWASP, networking.
2. Intermediate: threat modelling, IAM, detection.
3. Advanced: cloud/AI security and offensive testing.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Critical vulns open
- MTTR for incidents
- Phishing/test pass rate
- Coverage of controls

## Typical Deliverables

- Threat models
- Pen-test reports
- Security controls
- Incident reports

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: DevSecOps, Privacy Engineer, Compliance Specialist, AI Safety Engineer.

## Career Progression

1. **Security Analyst**
2. **Security Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **Security Architect / CISO**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. Threat-model this feature for me.
2. How do you defend an LLM app against prompt injection?
3. Walk through your incident-response process.
