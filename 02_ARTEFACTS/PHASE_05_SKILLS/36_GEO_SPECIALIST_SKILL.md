## Role Summary

**Mission:** Ensure AI answer engines surface, cite and recommend the brand accurately and favourably.

A Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Specialist makes a brand and its content visible and accurately represented inside AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot and similar. As people increasingly ask AI assistants instead of clicking blue links, GEO is the discipline that ensures the AI mentions, cites and recommends you correctly. It is the natural evolution of SEO for the generative-AI era.

## Key Responsibilities

- Optimise content so AI engines cite and quote it accurately.
- Build authoritative, well-structured, fact-dense content with clear claims.
- Establish brand entity presence (Wikipedia, Wikidata, knowledge graph, schema).
- Monitor how AI assistants describe and cite the brand; correct inaccuracies.
- Earn citations on the third-party sources AI models trust and quote.
- Maintain machine-readable facts (structured data, llms.txt, fact sheets).
- Track share-of-voice and sentiment across AI answer engines.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Structured data (schema.org), entity and knowledge-graph optimisation.
- Content structuring for LLM extractability (clear claims, citations, stats).
- Understanding retrieval and how LLMs select/cite sources.
- Technical content hygiene: crawlability, llms.txt, canonical facts.

### Business Skills

- Brand authority, digital PR and citation building.
- Competitive share-of-voice analysis in AI answers.
- Aligning GEO to demand generation and reputation.

### AI Skills

- How generative engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) retrieve and cite.
- Prompt-testing brand queries and measuring AI responses.
- Distinguishing model training data vs. live retrieval (RAG) influence.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Perplexity / ChatGPT / Gemini (testing)
- Profound / Otterly / Peec AI (AI-visibility tracking)
- Schema markup tools
- Search Console
- Ahrefs/Semrush
- Wikidata / knowledge-graph tools

## Certifications and Qualifications

- SEO/content marketing foundations
- AIBOS GEO Specialist certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: how LLMs and AI search work; SEO/AEO basics; structured data.
2. Intermediate: entity optimisation, citation building, AI-visibility tracking.
3. Advanced: cross-engine GEO strategy, measurement and reputation management.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Share-of-voice / mention rate in AI answers for target queries.
- Citation count and accuracy across AI engines.
- Sentiment/accuracy of AI-generated brand descriptions.
- Referral traffic and conversions from AI assistants.

## Typical Deliverables

- GEO strategy and target-query map.
- AI-optimised, citable content and fact sheets.
- Entity/knowledge-graph and structured-data improvements.
- AI-visibility monitoring dashboard and remediation log.

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: SEO Specialist, AEO Specialist, Content Strategist, Knowledge Manager, Growth Marketer.

## Career Progression

1. **SEO/Content Specialist**
2. **GEO Specialist**
3. **Senior GEO Specialist**
4. **Head of AI Search / Organic Growth**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. What is GEO and how does it differ from SEO and AEO?
2. How would you get a brand cited correctly by ChatGPT or Perplexity?
3. How do you measure brand visibility inside AI answer engines?
4. An AI assistant states a false fact about our product — what do you do?
5. How do entities and knowledge graphs influence AI answers?
