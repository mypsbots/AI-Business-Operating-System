## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** partnership document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. Partnership, alliance, reseller, revenue-share and joint-venture arrangements can raise competition-law, tax, employment, agency and unintended-liability issues — obtain solicitor sign-off for exclusive, equity, cross-border, competition-sensitive, high-value or otherwise complex deals before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Revenue Share and Commission Schedule sets out exactly how money flows in a partnership: the base amount the share/commission is calculated on, the rate(s), the qualifying events that trigger payment, the exclusions (refunds, taxes, chargebacks, churn), and the reporting, reconciliation and audit mechanics.

Putting the economics in a schedule keeps the parent agreement stable and makes disputes rare by removing ambiguity about who gets paid what, when.

## When to Use

- Attached to an Alliance, Reseller or Referral Agreement.
- Wherever a margin, commission, or revenue/profit share must be defined precisely.
- Where tiered rates, caps or minimum commitments apply.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. State the binding status explicitly, keep the parties' obligations balanced, and stay consistent with the [UK Partnership Framework](00_UK_PARTNERSHIP_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parent agreement reference** — The agreement this schedule attaches to and takes its defined terms from.
2. **Economic model** — Margin/discount, commission, or revenue/profit share — state which.
3. **Base amount** — Exactly what the share/commission is calculated on (e.g. net revenue excluding VAT, refunds).
4. **Rate(s) and tiers** — The percentage(s), any volume tiers, accelerators or caps.
5. **Qualifying event** — What triggers entitlement (order signed, payment received, go-live).
6. **Exclusions and adjustments** — Refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, taxes, bad debt, churn clawbacks.
7. **VAT and invoicing** — VAT treatment; who invoices whom and when.
8. **Reporting and statements** — Cadence and content of statements; self-billing (if used).
9. **Reconciliation and disputes** — How discrepancies are raised and resolved.
10. **Audit rights** — Right to inspect records supporting the calculation.
11. **Minimum commitments** — Any minimums, and consequences of missing them (if applicable).
12. **Payment terms** — Payment days and method.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- The parent agreement and its defined terms.
- Economic model, base amount, rate(s)/tiers and qualifying event.
- Exclusions, VAT treatment, reporting cadence and audit rights.
- Any minimum commitments and payment terms.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Define the base amount with no ambiguity (net of VAT, refunds and taxes) — most revenue-share disputes are about the base.
- Get the VAT treatment of commissions/revenue shares right; confirm who issues the VAT invoice (or whether self-billing is used).
- Avoid any term that fixes a reseller's resale price — set the share, not the customer price.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Worked examples in the schedule prevent 90% of disputes.
- Tie clawbacks to refunds/churn so you never pay commission on money you repay.
- Keep reporting and audit rights concrete and time-bound.
