## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** partnership document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. Partnership, alliance, reseller, revenue-share and joint-venture arrangements can raise competition-law, tax, employment, agency and unintended-liability issues — obtain solicitor sign-off for exclusive, equity, cross-border, competition-sensitive, high-value or otherwise complex deals before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Reseller (or Channel Partner) Agreement lets a partner sell the company's product or SaaS to end customers, defining whether the partner resells in its own name or refers, the discount/margin or price, the territory and any exclusivity, the partner's sales and support obligations, brand use, and end-customer terms.

It grows distribution while protecting the vendor's brand, pricing integrity (within competition-law limits), customers and IP.

## When to Use

- A partner will sell your product/SaaS to its own customers.
- Building a channel/distribution network or regional coverage.
- Where margin, territory and support responsibilities must be defined.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. State the binding status explicitly, keep the parties' obligations balanced, and stay consistent with the [UK Partnership Framework](00_UK_PARTNERSHIP_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parties and appointment** — Appoint the reseller; state exclusive or non-exclusive and the territory.
2. **Products and resale model** — What may be resold and whether the reseller resells in its own name or as an agent.
3. **Pricing, discount and margin** — Vendor price and reseller discount/margin; the reseller sets its own resale price (RPM caution).
4. **Orders and provisioning** — How the reseller orders, how customers are provisioned and activated.
5. **Reseller obligations** — Sales effort, minimum commitments (if any), accreditation/training, first-line support.
6. **Vendor obligations** — Product supply, updates, second-line support, enablement materials.
7. **End-customer terms and EULA** — The customer must accept the vendor's end-user terms; flow-down obligations.
8. **Brand and trade-mark licence** — Controlled use of the vendor's marks; brand guidelines and approval.
9. **Intellectual property** — No IP transfer; licence only to market and resell.
10. **Data protection** — UK GDPR roles for customer data; DPA/Data Sharing Agreement as applicable.
11. **Anti-bribery and lawful sales** — Bribery Act 2010 compliance; no misrepresentation; honest marketing.
12. **Warranties and liability** — Product warranty scope; caps and excluded losses; non-excludable liabilities preserved.
13. **Term, renewal and termination** — Duration; termination for cause/convenience/insolvency/change of control.
14. **Post-termination** — Run-off of existing customers, trailing margin, customer transition, licence survival.
15. **Governing law** — England and Wales.
16. **Signatures** — Authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Products/SaaS to be resold; exclusive or non-exclusive; territory.
- Pricing, discount/margin and any minimum commitments.
- Support split; brand-use rules; end-customer terms.
- Term, renewal and post-termination treatment of customers.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Resale price maintenance (fixing the reseller's resale price or minimum price) is generally unlawful under UK competition law — the reseller must be free to set its resale price; you may set a recommended price only.
- Exclusivity and territorial restrictions can engage the Competition Act 1998 — review before granting.
- Reseller commissions/discounts carry Bribery Act 2010 risk — include ABC warranties and audit rights.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Set a recommended (not mandatory) resale price; let the reseller price freely.
- Define support tiers clearly (first-line reseller, second-line vendor).
- Protect existing customers with run-off and transition terms on exit.
