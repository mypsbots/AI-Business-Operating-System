Partnership, alliance, reseller, revenue-share and joint-venture arrangements can raise competition-law, tax, employment, agency and unintended-liability issues — obtain solicitor sign-off for exclusive, equity, cross-border, competition-sensitive, high-value or otherwise complex deals before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Partnership Governance / Joint Steering Committee Terms of Reference sets out how an ongoing alliance or JV is run: who sits on the steering committee, how often it meets, what it decides, the KPIs it tracks, how issues escalate, and how the partnership is reviewed and changed.

Good governance keeps a partnership active and accountable, prevents drift, and provides a structured way to resolve issues before they become disputes.

## When to Use

- Any ongoing alliance or JV that needs active management.
- As a schedule to the Strategic Partnership or Joint Venture Agreement.
- Where KPIs, decision rights and escalation must be defined.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. State the binding status explicitly, keep the parties' obligations balanced, and stay consistent with the [UK Partnership Framework](00_UK_PARTNERSHIP_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Purpose and scope** — What the governance body oversees.
2. **Steering committee membership** — Named roles/representatives from each party and a chair.
3. **Meeting cadence and quorum** — How often it meets, quorum and how meetings are called.
4. **Decision-making and thresholds** — How decisions are made; matters needing joint or escalated approval.
5. **KPIs and success metrics** — The measures tracked and the targets.
6. **Reporting** — What is reported, by whom and when.
7. **Issue management and escalation** — How operational issues are raised and escalated to leadership.
8. **Change mechanism** — How the partnership scope/plan is changed without re-papering the whole agreement.
9. **Review points** — Periodic health-check and renewal reviews.
10. **Dispute escalation** — Escalation ladder before formal dispute resolution.
11. **Records** — Minutes, decisions and action tracking.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Parent agreement reference; committee representatives and chair.
- Meeting cadence, quorum and decision thresholds.
- KPIs/success metrics and reporting cadence.
- Escalation ladder and review points.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Keep governance decisions within the authority each agreement grants — the committee should not be able to bind beyond its remit.
- For JVs, align decision thresholds with the reserved-matters list in the JV Agreement.
- Record decisions in minutes; they can matter if a dispute arises.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Pick a few meaningful KPIs rather than many vanity metrics.
- Give the committee a clear change mechanism so the partnership can evolve.
- Define escalation so issues reach decision-makers quickly.
