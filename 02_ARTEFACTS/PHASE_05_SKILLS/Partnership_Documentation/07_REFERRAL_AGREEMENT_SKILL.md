## Legal Notice

> **Legal notice.** This skill produces a **draft** partnership document to speed up preparation. It is **not legal advice** and is **not a substitute for review by a UK-qualified solicitor**. Partnership, alliance, reseller, revenue-share and joint-venture arrangements can raise competition-law, tax, employment, agency and unintended-liability issues — obtain solicitor sign-off for exclusive, equity, cross-border, competition-sensitive, high-value or otherwise complex deals before signing. Unless otherwise agreed, the governing law is **England and Wales**. Do not store passwords, API keys or production credentials in generated documents.

## Purpose

A Referral (or Introducer) Agreement rewards a partner for introducing prospective customers that go on to buy, defining what counts as a qualifying introduction, the commission, how long referrals are tracked, and the limits on the referrer's role (introduce only — not resell, advise, or bind the company).

It is the lightest-touch channel model: the company contracts directly with the customer, and the referrer is paid for the introduction.

## When to Use

- A partner will pass leads but not sell, advise or contract with the customer.
- Low-friction channel or affiliate arrangements.
- Where commission on converted introductions is the agreed reward.

## Document Blueprint (Generation Template)

When this skill is invoked, generate the document by expanding each blueprint section below into clear, formal UK business English, filling every placeholder from the *Inputs Required*, resolving or flagging any missing input, marking the output as a draft, and appending a dated signature block for the authorised signatories. State the binding status explicitly, keep the parties' obligations balanced, and stay consistent with the [UK Partnership Framework](00_UK_PARTNERSHIP_FRAMEWORK.md).

1. **Parties and role** — Appoint the referrer to introduce leads only; no authority to bind, advise or resell.
2. **Qualifying introduction** — What counts as a valid, trackable introduction (named contact, consent, registration).
3. **Referral process and tracking** — How referrals are submitted, acknowledged and tracked; duplicate/conflict handling.
4. **Attribution window** — How long after introduction a conversion earns commission.
5. **Commission** — Rate, base amount, qualifying event (signed/paid), exclusions (refunds, taxes, churn) — link to Revenue Share Schedule.
6. **Payment and reporting** — Invoicing, VAT treatment, payment timing, statements and audit rights.
7. **Data protection** — Lawful basis for sharing contact data; consent/legitimate interests; transparency to the lead.
8. **Anti-bribery** — Bribery Act 2010 compliance; no inducements to improper conduct; honest representation.
9. **No agency / no authority** — Referrer cannot make representations, give advice or bind the company.
10. **Confidentiality** — Protect company and customer information.
11. **Term and termination** — Duration; treatment of in-flight referrals and trailing commission on exit.
12. **Governing law** — England and Wales.
13. **Signatures** — Authorised signatories and dates.

## Inputs Required to Generate

- Commission rate, base and qualifying event; attribution window.
- Referral submission/tracking process and exclusions.
- Data-sharing basis for lead contact details.
- Term and trailing-commission treatment.

## UK Legal and Compliance Notes

- Check whether the arrangement makes the partner a 'commercial agent' under the Commercial Agents (Council Directive) Regulations 1993 — a true agent may be entitled to compensation/indemnity on termination; a mere introducer usually is not.
- Sharing a lead's personal data needs a lawful basis and transparency under UK GDPR — do not pass contact details without this.
- Referral commissions are a common Bribery Act 2010 risk, especially in regulated sectors — include ABC terms.

## Best Practices and Pitfalls

- Define 'qualifying introduction' and the attribution window precisely to avoid commission disputes.
- Keep the referrer as introducer-only, with no authority to advise or bind.
- Confirm the lead has been told their details may be shared.
