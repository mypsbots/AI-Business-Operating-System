## Role Summary

**Mission:** Give LLMs accurate, up-to-date, grounded knowledge with citations.

Builds Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems — connecting LLMs to your knowledge so answers are grounded, current and citable.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design ingestion, chunking and embedding pipelines.
- Build retrieval (vector/hybrid/rerank) and grounding.
- Implement citations and reduce hallucination.
- Evaluate retrieval and answer quality.
- Optimise cost, freshness and latency.

## Required Skills

### Technical Skills

- Embeddings, vector databases and chunking strategies.
- Hybrid search and re-ranking.
- Evaluation (faithfulness, recall, precision).
- Data pipelines and freshness.

### Business Skills

- Mapping knowledge sources to use cases.

### AI Skills

- Grounding, citation and hallucination mitigation; RAG evals.

### Soft Skills

- Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical people.
- Structured problem solving and evidence-based decision making.
- Collaboration across human teammates and AI agents.
- Ownership, reliability and a continuous-learning mindset.

## Tools and Technologies

- Pinecone/pgvector/Weaviate
- LlamaIndex/LangChain
- Rerankers (Cohere)
- Ragas

## Certifications and Qualifications

- Vector DB certs
- AIBOS RAG Engineering certification (Phase 17)

## Learning Path

1. Foundational: embeddings, vector search, prompting.
2. Intermediate: chunking, hybrid search, reranking, evals.
3. Advanced: production RAG, graph RAG, optimisation.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Answer faithfulness
- Retrieval recall/precision
- Citation accuracy
- Latency/cost

## Typical Deliverables

- RAG pipeline
- Eval reports
- Ingestion connectors

## Collaboration and Interfaces

This role works most closely with: LLM Engineer, Data Engineer, Knowledge Manager.

## Career Progression

1. **ML/Backend Engineer**
2. **RAG Engineer**
3. **Senior**
4. **AI Architect**

## Sample Interview Questions

1. How do you chunk documents for retrieval?
2. How do you measure if RAG answers are grounded?
3. How do you keep a RAG knowledge base fresh?
