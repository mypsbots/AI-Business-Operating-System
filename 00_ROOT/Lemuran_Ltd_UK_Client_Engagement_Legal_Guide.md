# Lemuran Ltd — UK Client Project Engagement Legal & Contracting Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Jurisdiction:** England and Wales  
**Prepared for:** Lemuran Ltd  
**Purpose:** Practical business guide for engaging customers for AI, automation, software, SaaS, consulting, implementation, and related technology projects.

> **Important legal notice:** This is a practical business guide, not a substitute for advice from a UK-qualified solicitor. Contracts should be reviewed by a solicitor before Lemuran Ltd uses them for high-value, regulated, international, security-sensitive, or complex projects.

---

## 1. The simple answer

When Lemuran Ltd takes on a customer project, both parties should not rely only on emails, verbal discussions, a proposal, or an invoice.

For a normal UK business-to-business project, the safest structure is:

1. **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement**, where confidential information will be shared before the commercial contract is signed.
2. **Proposal or quotation**, explaining the solution, price, assumptions, and validity period.
3. **Master Services Agreement**, setting the general legal rules for the relationship.
4. **Statement of Work**, describing the exact project, deliverables, timeline, price, and acceptance criteria.
5. **Data Processing Agreement**, where Lemuran Ltd processes personal data for the customer.
6. **Service Level Agreement**, where Lemuran Ltd provides ongoing support, hosting, managed services, or availability commitments.
7. **Change Request**, for any agreed change to scope, price, timeline, or deliverables.
8. **Acceptance or Completion Certificate**, confirming that the customer has accepted the deliverables.
9. **Invoice and payment records**, supported by the signed contract and agreed milestones.

For a small, low-risk project, some of these can be combined into one well-drafted **Services Agreement** with schedules.

# 3. Core documents

## 3.1 Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

### Purpose

A Mutual NDA protects confidential information shared by both Lemuran Ltd and the prospective customer.

### Use it when

- discussing a customer’s internal processes;
- reviewing source code, credentials, datasets, financial information, customer records, product plans, or architecture;
- presenting Lemuran Ltd’s confidential methods, reusable assets, pricing logic, or product roadmap;
- speaking with a subcontractor or implementation partner.

### Important clauses

- definition of confidential information;
- exclusions, such as information already public;
- permitted use;
- persons allowed to receive the information;
- security obligations;
- legally required disclosure;
- return or deletion of information;
- duration of confidentiality;
- no transfer of intellectual property;
- remedies for breach;
- governing law and courts.

### Practical note

An NDA protects secrecy, but it does not replace the main services contract.

## 3.2 Proposal or Quotation

### Purpose

The proposal explains what Lemuran Ltd recommends and the commercial offer.

### It should contain

- customer problem and desired outcome;
- proposed solution;
- scope summary;
- deliverables;
- assumptions;
- exclusions;
- expected timeline;
- fees and VAT position;
- payment schedule;
- proposal validity period;
- dependencies on the customer;
- optional services;
- link or reference to the contract terms;
- statement that work will not begin until the agreement is signed and any required deposit is paid.

### Legal status

A proposal can become contractually binding if it contains sufficiently definite terms and is accepted. Therefore, mark it clearly as either:

- **“Subject to contract”**, when it is not intended to be binding; or
- **“Accepted as part of the Agreement”**, when it will form part of the signed contract.

### Recommended governing law

For Lemuran Ltd’s standard UK contracts:

> **The agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.**

International customers may request arbitration or another jurisdiction. Lemuran Ltd should obtain legal advice before agreeing.

## 3.4 Statement of Work

### Purpose

The SOW defines exactly what Lemuran Ltd will deliver for one project.

### Essential sections

- SOW reference number;
- effective date;
- related MSA;
- background and objectives;
- detailed scope;
- deliverables;
- excluded items;
- milestones;
- project plan;
- customer dependencies;
- customer-provided materials;
- delivery method and location;
- personnel and roles;
- assumptions;
- fees;
- expenses;
- payment milestones;
- acceptance criteria;
- testing process;
- acceptance period;
- deemed acceptance rule;
- support or warranty period;
- intellectual-property treatment;
- data-processing details;
- special security requirements;
- signatories.

### Why acceptance criteria matter

Avoid vague phrases such as:

> “The system will work well.”

Use measurable criteria, for example:

> “The workflow will process the approved test file and create the specified output fields with no Severity 1 defects.”

## 3.5 Data Processing Agreement

A DPA is required where Lemuran Ltd processes personal data **on behalf of** the customer as a processor.

The UK GDPR requires a binding controller-processor contract containing mandatory provisions. These include processing only on documented instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, assistance with rights requests, breach support, deletion or return of data, and audit information.

### DPA information schedule

Record:

- subject matter of processing;
- duration;
- nature and purpose;
- categories of personal data;
- categories of data subjects;
- controller rights and obligations;
- approved sub-processors;
- data locations;
- international transfer mechanism;
- retention period;
- technical and organisational measures.

### Typical situations requiring a DPA

- customer shares employee or customer records;
- Lemuran Ltd builds or operates an AI system using personal data;
- Lemuran Ltd hosts customer data;
- Lemuran Ltd accesses a CRM, email system, help desk, HR platform, or analytics environment;
- support work provides access to identifiable user information.

### Controller-to-controller sharing

Where both parties decide independently why and how personal data is used, a processor DPA may not be correct. A **data sharing agreement** may be needed instead.

## 3.6 Service Level Agreement

Use an SLA when Lemuran Ltd provides an ongoing managed service, hosting, maintenance, support, or availability commitment.

### Include

- service hours;
- support channels;
- priority definitions;
- response times;
- target resolution or workaround times;
- uptime target;
- maintenance windows;
- exclusions;
- customer responsibilities;
- monitoring method;
- service credits;
- reporting;
- escalation path;
- security incident contact;
- disaster recovery and business continuity commitments.

### Avoid overpromising

Do not promise 100% uptime. Define what is excluded, such as:

- planned maintenance;
- customer systems;
- internet failures outside Lemuran Ltd’s control;
- third-party cloud or AI provider failures;
- force majeure;
- customer misuse.

# 4. Supporting documents

## 4.1 Change Request

No material change should be accepted only through a call or informal message.

The Change Request should state:

- requested change;
- reason;
- effect on scope;
- effect on price;
- effect on timeline;
- effect on resources;
- technical or security consequences;
- revised acceptance criteria;
- authorisation by both parties.

A clear clause should say that Lemuran Ltd is not required to start changed work until the Change Request is approved.

## 4.2 Project Plan

Useful for larger projects. Include:

- work breakdown;
- milestones;
- owners;
- dependencies;
- risks;
- communication cadence;
- reporting;
- decision deadlines.

## 4.3 Requirements Specification

For development or automation work, document:

- functional requirements;
- non-functional requirements;
- integrations;
- environments;
- data inputs and outputs;
- roles and permissions;
- performance requirements;
- security requirements;
- testing requirements;
- regulatory constraints.

## 4.4 Security Schedule

Recommended where Lemuran Ltd handles sensitive information, privileged system access, or enterprise systems.

It can cover:

- access control;
- multifactor authentication;
- encryption;
- vulnerability management;
- logging;
- backups;
- incident handling;
- personnel security;
- secure development;
- segregation of customer environments;
- data deletion;
- business continuity;
- penetration testing;
- customer audit rights.

## 4.5 AI Schedule or Responsible AI Addendum

Recommended for AI-related work.

Include:

- intended and prohibited uses;
- customer responsibility for human review;
- accuracy and hallucination limitations;
- model and provider dependencies;
- training-data and input-data rights;
- treatment of prompts and outputs;
- personal-data restrictions;
- confidential-data restrictions;
- bias and fairness testing;
- explainability expectations;
- model changes;
- output ownership and licensing;
- regulatory responsibilities;
- logging and monitoring;
- safety controls;
- customer acceptance of residual risks.

## 4.6 Acceptance Certificate

At project completion, obtain written confirmation stating:

- deliverables received;
- testing completed;
- outstanding minor items;
- effective acceptance date;
- start of warranty or support;
- approval to issue final invoice.

## 4.7 Purchase Order

Some customers issue a PO before work begins. A PO is not a replacement for Lemuran Ltd’s contract.

The agreement should state which document takes priority if the customer’s PO contains conflicting terms.

## 4.8 Invoice

Each invoice should include:

- Lemuran Ltd’s legal name;
- company address;
- company number where appropriate;
- invoice number;
- invoice date;
- customer details;
- description of services;
- SOW or PO reference;
- net amount;
- VAT details, only where applicable;
- total due;
- payment terms;
- bank details;
- late-payment wording where appropriate.

# 5. Intellectual property

## 5.1 Do not assume the customer owns everything automatically

For commissioned work in the UK, the creator is often the first copyright owner unless a written agreement transfers ownership. A copyright assignment must be in writing and signed by the copyright owner.

Therefore, the contract must clearly separate:

### Background IP

Anything Lemuran Ltd owned, developed, licensed, or used before the project, including:

- frameworks;
- templates;
- libraries;
- prompts;
- reusable code;
- tools;
- processes;
- know-how;
- models;
- connectors;
- documentation structures.

Lemuran Ltd should normally retain ownership of its Background IP.

### Project-specific deliverables

The contract should state whether the customer receives:

- full ownership through assignment;
- a perpetual licence;
- a term-limited licence;
- an internal-use licence;
- a licence restricted to named systems or users.

### Third-party materials

Identify:

- open-source components;
- commercial software;
- cloud services;
- AI models;
- third-party APIs;
- stock assets.

Lemuran Ltd cannot give the customer greater rights than Lemuran Ltd itself has.

## 5.2 Recommended commercial approach

For most agency projects:

- Lemuran Ltd retains Background IP.
- The customer receives ownership or a broad licence to the bespoke paid-for deliverables.
- Lemuran Ltd retains generic ideas, skills, know-how, tools, and reusable components.
- Assignment or final licence becomes effective only after full payment.
- Third-party and open-source components remain subject to their own licences.

# 6. Payment and financial protections

## 6.1 Recommended payment structure

For a new customer:

- 30%–50% deposit before starting;
- milestone payments during delivery;
- final balance on acceptance or delivery;
- recurring services paid monthly or annually in advance.

For a short project, 50% upfront and 50% before production release may be suitable.

## 6.2 Payment terms

Use clear terms such as:

> “Invoices are payable within 14 days of the invoice date.”

## 6.3 Late payment

For qualifying UK business-to-business debts, statutory interest is generally 8% above the Bank of England base rate, together with potential fixed compensation. Contract wording should be reviewed because a contractual interest clause can affect statutory rights.

## 6.4 Suspension

The contract should allow Lemuran Ltd to suspend work or services after:

- payment becomes overdue;
- written notice is given;
- a reasonable cure period expires;
- continued work would create legal, security, or operational risk.

## 6.5 Expenses

State whether:

- fees include expenses;
- pre-approval is required;
- travel is charged;
- third-party costs are passed through;
- foreign exchange and bank charges are included.

# 7. Liability and risk allocation

## 7.1 Liability cap

A common B2B approach is to cap ordinary contractual liability at a stated amount, for example:

- fees paid or payable under the relevant SOW;
- fees paid in the previous 12 months;
- a negotiated fixed sum.

The appropriate cap depends on project value and risk.

## 7.2 Excluded losses

Contracts often exclude, where legally permitted:

- indirect or consequential loss;
- lost profits;
- lost revenue;
- lost business;
- loss of anticipated savings;
- loss of goodwill;
- data loss, subject to negotiation.

## 7.3 Matters that should not be improperly excluded

UK law may prevent or restrict exclusion of certain liabilities, including:

- death or personal injury caused by negligence;
- fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
- liabilities that cannot legally be limited.

A solicitor should draft this section.

## 7.4 Indemnities

Use indemnities carefully. They can create uncapped or unusually broad exposure.

Possible indemnity topics include:

- customer-provided content;
- customer instructions;
- third-party IP claims;
- misuse of the deliverables;
- data-protection breach;
- employment or subcontractor claims.

---

# 8. Warranties and AI-specific limitations

Lemuran Ltd may warrant that it will:

- perform services with reasonable skill and care;
- use appropriately qualified personnel;
- substantially follow the agreed specification;
- comply with applicable law in performing its own obligations;
- have the right to enter the agreement.

For AI systems, state clearly that:

- outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or non-unique;
- AI output requires appropriate human review;
- third-party models may change;
- results depend on customer data and instructions;
- no legal, medical, financial, or regulated decision should be made solely from AI output unless expressly agreed and appropriately governed;
- performance metrics must be defined in the SOW rather than assumed.

# 9. Customer responsibilities

The contract should require the customer to:

- provide accurate information;
- provide timely decisions and approvals;
- make systems and personnel available;
- obtain required licences and consents;
- have lawful rights to all supplied data and materials;
- maintain backups unless Lemuran Ltd agrees otherwise;
- use deliverables lawfully;
- follow security instructions;
- conduct acceptance testing;
- appoint an authorised project contact;
- pay invoices on time.

Customer delay should entitle Lemuran Ltd to adjust the timeline and, where appropriate, charge additional fees.

---

# 10. Subcontractors and third-party providers

Lemuran Ltd may use specialists, cloud providers, AI providers, hosting companies, or automation platforms.

The contract should address:

- right to use subcontractors;
- Lemuran Ltd’s responsibility for subcontracted work;
- confidentiality obligations;
- data protection and sub-processor approval;
- international data transfers;
- third-party service terms;
- replacement of providers;
- impact of provider outages or changes.

Maintain an internal register of approved subcontractors and sub-processors.

---

# 11. Consumer customers versus business customers

This guide mainly covers **business-to-business** projects.

Where the customer is an individual acting mainly outside their trade or profession, UK consumer law creates additional protections. Consumer terms must be fair and transparent, and cancellation rights may apply to distance contracts.

Lemuran Ltd should use separate consumer terms and obtain legal advice before selling bespoke services directly to consumers.

---

# 12. Signing and authority

Before signing, confirm:

- full legal names of both parties;
- company numbers;
- registered addresses;
- signatory names and titles;
- signatories have authority;
- all schedules are attached;
- blanks are completed;
- tracked changes are resolved;
- document version is final;
- effective date is stated;
- order of precedence is clear.

Electronic signatures are generally valid for commercial contracts in England and Wales, subject to any special formalities. Use a reputable electronic-signature platform with an audit trail for important agreements.

Discuss with a UK commercial insurance broker:

- Professional Indemnity Insurance;
- Cyber Insurance;
- Public Liability Insurance;
- Employers’ Liability Insurance, if legally required;
- Directors’ and Officers’ Insurance;
- Legal Expenses Insurance.

# 16. Internal contract approval rules for Lemuran Ltd

Create an internal approval matrix.

## Director approval required where

- project value exceeds the internal threshold;
- unlimited liability is requested;
- liability cap exceeds insurance;
- customer requests ownership of Lemuran Ltd Background IP;
- customer requests exclusivity;
- customer requests broad indemnities;
- sensitive or special-category personal data is involved;
- international transfers are complex;
- regulated decision-making is involved;
- customer requires security certifications not held;
- payment terms exceed the internal limit;
- foreign law or foreign courts apply;
- subcontracting is prohibited;
- source code escrow is required;
- penalties or service credits are unusually high.

# 17. Contract hierarchy

A recommended order of precedence is:

1. Signed Change Request
2. Statement of Work
3. Data Processing Agreement for data-protection matters
4. Service Level Agreement for service-level matters
5. Master Services Agreement
6. Proposal
7. Purchase Order

The contract should expressly state the hierarchy.

# 21. Practical operating rules

1. Do not start work without a signed agreement.
2. Do not start changed work without an approved Change Request.
3. Collect a deposit for new or higher-risk customers.
4. Define acceptance objectively.
5. Keep reusable IP outside customer ownership.
6. Do not process customer personal data without the correct data terms.
7. Do not accept unlimited liability without specialist legal review.
8. Keep all signed versions and audit trails.
9. Review the contract against insurance.
10. Use a solicitor for high-risk or high-value engagements.

# 23. Final recommendation for Lemuran Ltd

For the majority of Lemuran Ltd’s B2B AI and software projects, use:

> **MSA + SOW + DPA where personal data is processed + SLA where ongoing service commitments apply.**

Support this with:

> **NDA before detailed disclosure, Change Requests during delivery, and an Acceptance Certificate at completion.**

Have a UK commercial solicitor produce or review the first reusable template pack. Once approved, Lemuran Ltd can reuse the structure consistently while tailoring each SOW, DPA schedule, SLA, and risk allocation to the specific customer and project.
