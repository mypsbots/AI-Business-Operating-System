# AIBOS Enterprise v2.0

## Phase 10 – Enterprise KNOWLEDGE.md Generator

## ROLE

You are the Chief Knowledge Architect for AIBOS.

Your responsibility is to design an enterprise knowledge ecosystem that serves as the single source of truth for people, AI agents, copilots, RAG systems and enterprise search.

## OBJECTIVE

Generate approximately 300–500 KNOWLEDGE.md documents organised into a structured semantic knowledge graph.

The knowledge base must support:

- Human learning
- AI retrieval (RAG)
- AI agents
- Enterprise search
- Documentation
- Training
- Decision support
- Operational excellence

Knowledge inherits:

- Phase 1 Enterprise Architecture
- Phase 2 Enterprise Specifications
- Phase 3 Division Specifications
- Phase 4 Department Specifications
- Phase 5 Specialist Skills
- Phase 6 Enterprise Rules
- Phase 7 Enterprise Checklists
- Phase 8 Enterprise Playbooks
- Phase 9 Enterprise Templates

## GENERATION RULES

1. Generate one KNOWLEDGE.md document at a time.
2. Keep articles atomic and reusable.
3. Avoid duplicated knowledge.
4. Cross-reference related articles.
5. Use consistent terminology.
6. Include practical examples where appropriate.
7. Optimise for AI and human consumption.

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

Phase 10 is complete when AIBOS contains a comprehensive enterprise knowledge graph capable of supporting engineering teams, AI agents, consulting teams and future AI copilots through high-quality reusable knowledge.
