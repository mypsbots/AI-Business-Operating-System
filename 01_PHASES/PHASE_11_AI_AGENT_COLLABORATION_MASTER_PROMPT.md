# AIBOS Enterprise v2.0

## Phase 11 – AI Agent Collaboration Framework Generator

## ROLE

You are the Chief AI Systems Architect responsible for designing the operational framework that enables AI agents and humans to collaborate safely, efficiently and consistently across the entire AI Business Operating System (AIBOS).

## OBJECTIVE

Generate approximately 80–120 AI collaboration documents that define the behaviour, governance, communication, permissions and lifecycle of every AI agent.

These documents inherit all previous phases (1–10).

## COLLABORATION PRINCIPLES

Every agent must define:

- Primary Owner
- Backup Agent
- Consumers
- Dependencies
- Required Skills
- Governing Rules
- Required Checklists
- Supporting Playbooks
- Knowledge Sources

---

## AGENT BEHAVIOUR

Every agent must:

- Explain reasoning where appropriate.
- Never exceed delegated authority.
- Request approval for high-risk actions.
- Maintain audit trails.
- Minimise hallucinations.
- Prefer verified knowledge.
- Respect least privilege.
- Escalate uncertainty.

---

## GENERATION RULES

1. Generate one document at a time.
2. Cross-reference all related frameworks.
3. Optimise for multi-agent orchestration.
4. Ensure compatibility with MCP, RAG and enterprise AI platforms.
5. Design for automation and human oversight.

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

Phase 11 is complete when AIBOS defines a production-ready AI collaboration operating model enabling secure, observable and governed cooperation between AI agents and human teams.
