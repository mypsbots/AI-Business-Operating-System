## GENERATION RULES

1. Generate one division specification at a time.
2. Every division inherits Phase 1 and Phase 2.
3. Do not duplicate responsibilities.
4. Clearly define ownership boundaries.
5. Cross-reference all dependent divisions.
6. Maintain enterprise consistency.

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

Phase 3 is complete only when every business division has a fully defined operating manual that can independently execute its responsibilities while collaborating seamlessly with all other divisions.
